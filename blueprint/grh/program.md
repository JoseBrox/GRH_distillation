# Full proof program

A proof-wide work program traced from the pinned source endpoint. Complete source imports and endpoint-field accounting do not constitute a full theorem-dependency or mathematical verification.

## Preferred target

One compatible arithmetic construction at each required scale, controlling an averaged discrepancy and explicit finite errors with a total exponent loss tending to zero on whole windows.

The complete coherent-family route remains an available supplier. Its separate Galois-construction supplier is replaced in the preferred route by the fully compared classical theorem; independent theta and geometric obligations are retained.

The eleven work packages below are responsibilities in a research program, not eleven already proved theorems or a claim of minimal logical independence.

### W01: Analytic conclusion from vanishing exponent loss

**Required result.** For a fixed target character, exclude right-half-plane zeros from subpower weighted positive-part integrals of a fixed local logarithmic average of positive prime-sum combinations, at exponents tending to one half.

**Quantifiers and uniformity.** For fixed j, H_j, its finite family and positive weights, M_j and b_j>1 precede the windows. They may vary with j; no uniform positive lower bound on the target coefficient is needed.

**Source interface.** `ChebyshevInequalityOnWindows`; `half-plane and reflection endpoints`.

**Existing manuscript coverage.** r02, r03, r04. Stable section ids are in the [inventory](../module-inventory.md).

**Next concrete task.** Use the proved averaged criterion as the final acceptance test. Keep each analytic family, averaging width and interval exponent fixed at one accuracy, and retain the same expanded cofinal windows for the arithmetic comparison and its integral bound.

**Acceptance conditions:**

- The target character or its conjugate occurs with positive coefficient in every H_j; H_j is fixed within its own window sequence
- The weighted positive-part integral is small on the same cofinal windows where the arithmetic comparison holds almost everywhere; fixed local averaging uses the expanded interval
- A signed long-window mean or an unweighted exceptional proportion is insufficient
- Every arithmetic loss is included in the final budget and total exponent loss

## Compatibility is part of the theorem

All fields share one familyclass A and selection sigma. Measures/maps/spaces referenced by selP,selB,selS must be transported coherently; separate constructions cannot be substituted without an equality/compatibility theorem.

RawSel on a possibly empty A is not sufficient. Retain hEx (MinkDiv implies ExS) and its classical MinkDiv input: ExS constructs admissible families under prescribed host requirements. Closed conditions and nRest must hold on those actual families.

A separately proved bound for some lattice, some theta map, or some measure does not discharge a field that refers to a different selected object. Every proposed substitution needs the relevant equality, transport or compatibility statement.

The [adopted classical Galois substitution](../../notes/classical-galois-substitution.md) supplies the universal source row. The full local laws, depth-zero types, local preimages and global period comparisons have passed independent review. The same global representation works for every allowed weak correspondence. The original Galois-construction supplier remains an alternative; independent theta identities, integral estimates and geometric bounds remain required.
