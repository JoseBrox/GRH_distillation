# Hecke GRH — independent extension program

This is the third research level of GRH_distillation, after
[ordinary RH](../paper/rh/main.pdf) and [Dirichlet GRH](../paper/grh/main.pdf).
We seek the critical-line assertion for every unitary Hecke character
over every number field, including infinite-order characters and
Dedekind zeta functions. This is stronger in scope than Alpöge's
verified Dirichlet result. We have not proved the new assertion.

## What has been achieved

A Hecke L-function records prime ideals and the values of a character
at them. Our one-sided analytic method reduces the zero problem to a
smoothed prime-sum estimate, as in our Dirichlet reconstruction.
A positive induction argument now reduces all unitary Hecke characters
to totally imaginary Galois fields over the rationals. At the decisive
square-root scale, we only need completely split rational primes,
weighted by the character. This works also for infinite-order characters.

The preferred route develops this arithmetic estimate independently
of Masullo. An alternative uses one-sided bounds for actual spectral
traces in Masullo's approach. We will use and credit relevant
RiemannMasullo work; neither route assumes that its gap is solved.

## What remains

The first nonabelian benchmark is the Dedekind zeta function of the
S3 normal closure of the cubic field generated by the cube root of 2.
The general weighted split-prime estimate, or a compatible geometric
trace supplier, remains open. Alpöge's theorem handles the rational
and certain abelian cases, but not this new arithmetic problem.
