# Limitations

- Genuine nonabelian finite-image prime coefficients cannot be reproduced
  by a finite linear combination of Dirichlet characters, by Chebotarev
  and the commutator subgroup. See [transfer, §7](../notes/masullo-transfer.md).
- The lower-window relative traces invariant under a common inner
  multiplier cannot detect its off-strip zeros. Different iterated
  cutoffs may give different trace limits; see [transfer, §8](../notes/masullo-transfer.md)
  and the credited endpoint-window calculations.
- Positivity of the arithmetic kernel does not make a nonzero continuous
  positive-definite convolution kernel vanishing at the identity.
  The two-by-two positive-definite inequality forbids the latter.
- Finite-order characters are trivial on the connected norm-one component;
  finite-order GRH does not handle arbitrary infinite-order characters
  by an automatic approximation. The Galois-field reduction retains
  the infinite-order phases.
- The original Dirichlet arithmetic suppliers and a qualitative finite-stage
  limit are not a quantitative estimate of the new weighted split-prime
  deficit.

- At 3, a one-dimensional residual or invariant cubic sector can
  have arbitrarily large integral length. The exact isogeny error
  is controlled, but the actual twisted lengths require a new
  estimate; see [exceptional primes, §4](../notes/cubic-exceptional-prime-comparison.md).
- In the j-zero cubic CM twist, no invariant symmetric principal
  theta line has a section nonvanishing at the origin. A
  three-line orbit bundle is a proved local alternative, not a
  scalar equivariant replacement; see [torsor, §3](../notes/cubic-torsor-realization.md).
- Tensor duplication and phase isometries preserve old norms but
  cannot yield the new cubic divisibilities without label-sensitive
  maps. Constant cubic weights annihilate both new characters.
  Arbitrarily imposing local Frobenius divisors merely lowers the
  patched source degree; it does not bound the prime discrepancy.
  See [torsor, §§4–5](../notes/cubic-torsor-realization.md).

- A single simultaneously optimal interpolation set cannot be assumed
  outside Q (Frączyk–Szumowicz, Theorem A). The bounded global test
  set uses different local optimal subsequences; ordinary
  Vandermonde energy estimates do not give the needed centered
  critical-scale bound. Convolution over all ideals with the ideal
  counting function instead of the rational floor kernel cancels
  the detecting poles exactly: zeta_E*(-zeta'_E/zeta_E)=-zeta'_E
  in Dirichlet-series notation. See [interpolation, §§2,6](../notes/global-interpolation-lattices.md).
- The augmented value/derivative graph projection can be an
  archimedean near-isometry while its integral image has a large
  index. Conditioning alone is not a lower bound on the relative
  saturated covolume. The missing lower bound in
  [derivative lattices](../notes/global-derivative-lattices.md)
  is substantive, not a change of norm.

These are obstructions to specific proposed proof steps, not to GRH.

- The bounded-order simultaneous cubic theta operator is obtained
  by coefficient CRT. Its height allowance pays for the whole
  imposed prime divisor, so the individual metric norm bound
  does not imply the critical-scale collective saving. See
  [theta coefficient interface, Sections 3--4](../notes/cubic-theta-coefficient-interface.md).
- At reducible points of the actual polarized cubic frame, the
  cross-block Selmer module is a cubic Rankin--Selberg twist,
  not a rational Dirichlet twist of the parent rank-two form.
  The parent's Kato argument cannot simply be substituted; see
  [polarized model, Section 5](../notes/cubic-polarized-selmer-model.md).
