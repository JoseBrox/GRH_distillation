# Finite class-rank certificates for cubic cyclotomic control

24 November 2026. This strengthens
[pure-cubic Selmer descent](pure-cubic-selmer-descent.md)
and the [auxiliary tower audit](cubic-automorphic-seed-frontier.md).
The needed nontrivial cubic Iwasawa components have an exact
finite-layer control formula when the cubic extension is inert
at the coefficient prime. A finite rank test then certifies
mu=0, and a zero initial component gives integral strict
Selmer vanishing at *every* twist weight. No numerical
class-group certificate for an actual moving auxiliary field
is claimed here.

## 1. Set-up and exact finite-layer comparison

Let ell>=5 be a coefficient prime, E an abelian number field
over Q containing F=Q(omega) and mu_ell, and L=K E with
K=F(cuberoot(2)). The cubic extension K/F is nontrivial
after restriction to E (K is nonabelian over Q).
Let E_infty/E be the cyclotomic Z_ell-extension,
L_infty=L E_infty, and Delta=Gal(L_infty/E_infty)=C3.
We assume that every prime over ell in L is totally
ramified in L_infty/L, which can always be arranged by
moving to a fixed sufficiently deep initial layer. The
same statement then holds at every later layer L_n.
Write Gamma=Gal(L_infty/L)=Z_ell, and let gamma be a
topological generator. The two groups Delta and Gamma
act independently since the degree-three and pro-ell
extensions are disjoint over E.

Write X for the Galois group of the maximal unramified
abelian pro-ell extension M_infty of L_infty. Equivalently
it is the norm inverse limit of the primary ideal class
groups; the two usual definitions agree by class field
theory and descent of finite unramified characters.
The classical Iwasawa structure theorem makes X a finitely
generated torsion Z_ell[[Gamma]]-module. Enlarge coefficients
to a finite unramified DVR O containing the third roots of
unity. For either nontrivial cubic character lambda,
write X_lambda for the corresponding component of
X tensor O, using the integral idempotent
(1/3) sum_delta lambda(delta)^(-1) delta.
The action of Delta on X is well-defined by conjugation:
two lifts to Gal(M_infty/E_infty) differ by an element
of X, which is abelian. It commutes with Gamma by the
same argument. Let A_n=Cl(L_n){ell}, and put


```math
g_{\lambda,n}=\dim_k (A_n\otimes O)_\lambda/\ell
```


where k is the residue field of O.

We prove the following exact control statement.
Let s_lambda be the multiplicity of lambda in the
permutation representation of Delta on the primes of L
over ell. Thus s_lambda counts the orbits of size three,
i.e. the primes of E over ell splitting completely in L/E.
Then


```math
(X_\lambda)_{\Gamma^{\ell^n}}\longrightarrow
 (A_n\otimes O)_\lambda
 \longrightarrow 0
 \tag{1}
```


is onto, and its kernel can be generated by at most
s_lambda elements over O. In particular when every prime
of E over ell is inert in L/E the map is an isomorphism.

**Proof.** Put H_n=Gal(M_infty/L_n). Its exact sequence
with kernel X and quotient Gamma^{ell^n} splits over
any inertia group at a prime above ell. Indeed the
quotient extension is totally ramified there, while
M_infty/L_infty is unramified, so the inertia group
maps isomorphically onto Gamma^{ell^n}. The assertion
on inertia follows at every finite local subextension
from the exact sequence of inertia groups, and then
by inverse limit. Abelianization therefore gives


```math
0\longrightarrow X_{\Gamma^{\ell^n}}
 \longrightarrow H_n^{\rm ab}
 \longrightarrow \Gamma^{\ell^n}\longrightarrow0.
```


The abelianization is the maximal abelian pro-ell
quotient; the commutator subgroup is closed here
because gamma^{ell^n}-1 has compact image on X.
The unramified quotient of H_n^{ab} is exactly A_n:
every finite unramified abelian ell-extension of L_n
base changes to an unramified abelian extension of
L_infty, so is contained in M_infty. Away from ell
there is no inertia to kill, and above ell there are
the inertia groups just described, one for each prime
of L_n. In the abelianization their images are
independent of the choice of extensions of the prime
to M_infty (conjugation by H_n is now trivial).
For each prime choose the unique generator mapping
to gamma^{ell^n}. The closed module generated by
these images is therefore a quotient of the
permutation Z_ell-module on the primes above ell:
Delta permutes its canonical generators, because it
acts trivially on Gamma. The number and Delta action
on these primes are the same at every layer, by
total ramification. Its lambda-component needs at
most s_lambda generators, while the nontrivial
lambda-part of the quotient Gamma^{ell^n} is zero.
Taking the exact integral character component now
gives (1) and its kernel bound. QED.

This proof accounts for the inertia correction rather
than assuming that all finite class groups are exactly
the coinvariants of the inverse limit. Only the cubic
inert range eliminates that correction.

## 2. Finite sufficient tests

The finitely generated k[[T]]-module
X_lambda/ell X_lambda has a nonnegative free rank r.
If the mu-invariant of X_lambda is positive then r>=1,
by the structure theorem; conversely r=0 means mu=0.
This can also be seen by localizing at (ell) in the
Iwasawa algebra. Since k[[T]] is a DVR, its free part
is a direct summand, and consequently


```math
\dim_k X_\lambda/(\ell,\gamma^{\ell^n}-1)
 \ \ge\ r\ell^n .
```


Here gamma^{ell^n}-1 is T^{ell^n} modulo ell.
Combining with (1) proves the finite criterion


```math
g_{\lambda,n}+s_\lambda<\ell^n
       \quad\Longrightarrow\quad \mu(X_\lambda)=0.
 \tag{2}
```


In particular the rank test becomes particularly small
when s_lambda=0. If g_{\lambda,0}=0 and s_lambda=0,
then X_lambda=0 by compact Nakayama: its quotient
by (ell,T) is zero. Both conclusions are exact, not
an extrapolation from stable ranks in several samples.

Under (2), finite generation over O follows from the
structure theorem, including finite torsion. Moreover
the number of O-generators is bounded by


```math
\dim_k X_\lambda/\ell
 \ \le\ g_{\lambda,n}+s_\lambda
          \qquad\text{if }g_{\lambda,n}+s_\lambda<\ell^n .
 \tag{3}
```


Indeed after r=0 write the finite-length k[[T]]-module
as a sum of cyclic factors k[[T]]/(T^a). Its quotient
by T^{ell^n} has length sum min(a,ell^n). If the
sum is less than ell^n, every a<ell^n and the quotient
has the full length. Compact Nakayama over O proves
the generator bound. Thus a single verified finite rank
supplies not just the vanishing of mu but the actual
uniform constant in the tower estimate.

## 3. Why the inert condition occurs naturally

Suppose the rational coefficient prime ell has 3-cycle
Frobenius in S3=Gal(K/Q), equivalently ell=1 mod 3 and
2^((ell-1)/3) != 1 mod ell. Each of the two primes of F
over ell is then inert in K/F. If the residue degrees
of E/F at those places are prime to three, every prime
of E over ell is inert in K E/E.

For example this is the case if the field E is obtained
by adjoining the rational 2-power host fields, the
auxiliary real quadratic field and real ell-power
fields of the parent, together with mu_ell, all with
their stated conductor assumptions. The unramified
residue degree is a product divisor of 2-powers
and ell-powers; the tame cyclotomic factor at ell
is totally ramified and contributes no residue degree.
The additional cyclotomic layers at ell are also
totally ramified after the fixed initial level.
Thus s_lambda=0 in this part of the actual cubic
auxiliary construction.

By the [pure-cubic rank identity](pure-cubic-selmer-descent.md),
write E=F B with B its maximal real subfield, and B_n
its corresponding cyclotomic layers. The equality
E=F B holds because E is abelian CM and contains F.
Complex conjugation exchanges the two nontrivial
cubic components. Hence for either lambda,


```math
g_{\lambda,n}
 =\dim_{\mathbf F_\ell}\operatorname{Cl}(C B_n)/\ell
     -\dim_{\mathbf F_\ell}\operatorname{Cl}(B_n)/\ell .
 \tag{4}
```


Here C=Q(cuberoot(2)) and the quotient by ell denotes
the primary class group's reduction. Equality follows
from exact prime-to-ell invariants under the
transposition fixing C and under S3. This allows the
certificate (2) to be tested on degree-[C B_n:Q]
fields, rather than on the degree-six cubic splitting
extension, and without an independent computation of
both cubic eigenspaces. One may even use just the
upper bound by the class rank of C B_n in (2).

## 4. Integral strict Selmer vanishing at all weights

Assume s_lambda=0 and X_lambda=0 for both signs of
lambda, for example by (2) with n=0 and g_{\lambda,0}=0.
Let rho be an actual rational 2-power host character
cut out by E, and suppose [E:F] is prime to ell.
Let O' be any finite coefficient DVR containing its
values and omega, fraction field E', and consider


```math
D=(E'/O')(\varepsilon^{-e}\rho^{-1}\lambda),
       \qquad e\ge1 .
```


Then


```math
S_F(D)=0.                 \tag{5}
```


To prove it, restrict to E. This is injective on
strict cohomology by the prime-to-ell degree.
The rho factor is now trivial. The cyclotomic
character is trivial on G_{L_infty} because E
contains mu_ell and L_infty contains all higher
ell-power roots of unity. Thus a strict class over
E restricts to a homomorphism on G_{L_infty}.
Its restrictions to every finite inertia are zero,
and at primes over ell to the full decomposition
groups are zero. It factors through X, and equivariance
under Delta is by lambda (the finite cubic quotient
can be lifted with cyclotomic value one, since its
order is prime to ell). The two nontrivial cubic
components of X are zero. So the restricted
homomorphism vanishes.

Inflation--restriction now places the class in
H^1(Delta x Gamma,D), where Delta acts through the
nontrivial cubic character and its nonidentity value
minus one is a unit in O'. Thus D^Delta=0 and all
positive-degree cohomology of Delta vanishes by
averaging. Hochschild--Serre gives H^1=0 for the
product. This proves (5) at every positive weight,
not merely the weights trivial on the tame cyclotomic
part. Scalar extension of X to O' introduces no
new cubic component.

If the actual E contains ell-power auxiliary fields
then the restriction from F need not be injective,
so (5) in that form is not asserted. The vanishing
over E still holds and the earlier tower generator
criterion is independent of this additional restriction.
For the strict groups themselves one can use the
prime-to-ell field E cut out by rho, F and mu_ell,
before adding any ell-power auxiliary fields.

## 5. An explicit reduction of the auxiliary tower obligation

In the original deep-tower method the abelian part of
the class-rank bound is known by Ferrero--Washington.
The new test (2), checked for the pure-cubic companion
of the actual fixed base field and coefficient prime,
supplies the two nontrivial cubic parts with explicit
generator bounds (3). The auxiliary real degree-ell
fields can be added *after* a finite-generation
certificate when they are cyclic ell-extensions
ramifying at finitely many primes of the cyclotomic
Z_ell-field: the elementary cyclic-ell extension
generator lemma of [the fixed-conductor Selmer window](cubic-fixed-conductor-selmer-window.md)
bounds the new generators by ell times the old
bound plus the number of ramified primes. In a
cyclotomic Z_ell-field, any finite set of rational
primes other than ell has finitely many primes over
it, by the infinite order of its Frobenius image
in the cyclotomic group. This preserves finite
generation, though the constant can depend on the
initial arithmetic datum. The real quadratic
auxiliary extension is not an ell-extension for
ell>=5 and must be included in the fixed field
before applying that argument.

This gives a finite arithmetic certificate for the
tower-rank input of the parent method at each
coefficient prime and residual character datum.
It does not establish that the test succeeds at
every required datum, nor construct the remaining
cubic automorphic seed, finite Hecke comparison,
or the critical-scale length estimate in their absence.
In particular it is not legitimate to call the
general nonabelian mu assertion proved. All identities
and implications above are ordinary proofs; no
uncertified class-group calculation is used.

## Later characterwise improvement and actual certificates

The [characterwise strict-control theorem](cubic-characterwise-strict-control.md) replaces the full cubic component by the actual finite adjacent character. The local correction is at most two and is zero at every high-dyadic marked prime, including cubic-split primes. It also proves the converse: eventual success of the finite rank test is equivalent to the corresponding mu=0 assertion. The [five-field certificates](pure-cubic-class-certificates.md) give actual verified quadratic sectors, not a proof of uniform moving success.
