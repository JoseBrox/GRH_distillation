# Exact AA period normalization (W08)

The [proof](../paper/shared/flag-aa-normalization.tex) supplies (GM11) on the original `MassDataTie` and concrete `bkU` datum, including the actual finite-level and row hypotheses. It also supplies (GM12), the generic mass bound with the improved height loss (H^{s(s-1)}).

The proof uses the classical unitary Tamagawa-number theorem (2) in place of the much longer Siegel–Weil stabilizer-volume argument. It uses the already proved primitive residue density, primitive row genus mass and exact reference unipotent covolume, with no asymptotic change of measures. The argument for equal adelic volumes uses only nondegenerate tuples and their prescribed quotient compatibility. The actual local frame orbit transitivity is proved by residue Witt extension and skew-adjoint lifting. The exact ordinary and typed box volumes, graded stabilizer index, primitive complete-frame fibre measure, and local pair-volume formula are checked independently. At infinity the proof gives both the complete-frame quotient gauge and the two-sided big-cell/terminal Haar formula in the original doubled planar measures. The final discriminant and level exponent identities are polynomial identities.

**External classical input.** M. Harris, J.-S. Li and C. M. Skinner, [p-Adic L-Functions for Unitary Shimura Varieties, I](https://www2.math.ethz.ch/EMIS/journals/DMJDMV/vol-coates/harris_li_skinner.pdf), Documenta Mathematica Extra Volume Coates (2006), 393–464, (0.2.1), gives the Tamagawa measure normalization and number 2. The argument applies the same quotient normalization to unitary groups of different positive ranks; alternatively use the general Tamagawa-number formula (R. E. Kottwitz, Tamagawa numbers, Ann. of Math. 127 (1988), 629–646). This is a cited standard theorem, not a newly proved Tamagawa theorem.

**Verification scope.** The exponent identities were checked exactly by SymPy; the proof includes the dyadic and split cases and distinguishes the Hilbert–Schmidt unitary volume from the raw coordinate volume. No full Lean replay or manuscript compilation. The analytic pinned measure comparison and uniform weighted orbital point sum remain; W08 is not closed.
