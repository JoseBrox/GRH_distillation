# W08: primitive local densities and generic amplitudes

**Status:** proved for the actual unramified or split finite-place models, in the original rank range, for the generic branch. [Shared mathematical proof](../paper/shared/flag-generic-local-amplitude.tex).

Put s=r+1, m=2s, n=k+2r+1, q=the residue cardinality, and P=q^(v(theta)+a+d), the original Hp. The corrected joint lattice is generated by u and (u'-u)/theta. The determinant valuation is a, and its discriminant module has order q^(2a). For a fixed datum and integral frame class the generic complement lattice is determined by the two quotient lattices and their level graph.

The proof gives:

- Exact primitive Gram density, uniformly between 3/4 and 5/4 for n>=2m+3, with no invertibility requirement on the reduction of the Gram matrix. The split case and residue characteristic 2 are included.
- Exact reciprocal-stabilizer mass of self-dual lattices with prescribed saturated intersection, with the index exponent n-m=k-1. This proves the original mass_trace estimate with improved constants. The same Haar scalar is retained for determinant-one adapted changes.
- Sum of integral frame-class weights at most P^(s(s-1)), with every individual weight at least one. This uses the actual linked integral unitriangular subgroup, the block leading minors, and quotient Haar measure.
- At most q^(2am) generic lattices per fixed datum and frame class, by anti-isometric discriminant gluing. The two stabilizer-weight unfoldings are exact, not unweighted counts.
- If e=s^2+3s, the generic squared-amplitude sum is at most P^e nn. For any occupied pairing class the generic mass is positive finite and nn <= 2 P^e ngen(C). At P=1 both comparisons are equalities with constant one.

These bounds imply the generic part of the local NGAt / ZAt bound (C1=39) and the original LocalLowerBoundAtCoset with C0=9. The proof does not give the nongeneric saving. No use of the global trace estimate or desired point-sum estimate occurs.
