## What is now supplied

lattice trivial at the bad integer and level, every compatible odd N,
every prescribed integral harmonic polynomial, and every scalar satisfying
the original two-determinant-ideal inclusion, one original integer

    Z = intZ = M^(f*(p+q)*n + DF) * |disc(E)|^DF

with beta=1, so its original hypothesis already contains the exact finite
torsion expression. There is no additional analytic theta identity assumed.

replaced by the other. The original beta_H factor is accounted for by

supplier. Constructing a spanning family of those sections and proving
its archimedean norm bound remain separate. Their proof is not inferred
from the new integral membership conclusion. Whole-node counts do not change.
