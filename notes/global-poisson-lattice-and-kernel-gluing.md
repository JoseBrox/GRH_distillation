# Global Poisson: the trace-dual index and positive-kernel gluing

This note isolates the arithmetic and analytic gluing in the W08 global
Poisson construction. All the lattices below are the ones prescribed by
the source, not auxiliary lattices chosen to improve the bound. In
particular the different and the index in the dual lattice must not be
discarded.

## 1. The global period lattice

Write E/F for the host CM extension, f=[F:Q], g=gS, d for the absolute
different of E, and rd=|disc(E)|^(1/(2f)). The host has E/F unramified
at the finite places (as in the source unramE). For the block indexed by
nu and s put

    I = g d^nu,
    L = {t in I : t-(-1)^s conjugate(t) in g I}.

These are exactly the two conditions in G3lat, once the local agreement
and local different-generator pins hold. Indeed at each finite place
one may use a generator in F of the extended different, since the
relative different is trivial. The local membership conditions of
laIsPeriod_of then say respectively membership in I and the indicated
congruence modulo gI. Membership in a fractional ideal is equivalent to
membership at every finite place. In the phase identity G1root the
local factorization g=gpart*oth and the prescribed uP=1/oth,
qE=gpart give uP/qE=1/g. The character agreement is essential here,
including its minus sign; there is no independent rechoice of a local
character.

We have gI ⊂ L ⊂ I. More precisely,

    [I:L] = [L:gI] = g^f.

To see this, d=d_F O_E, so I is extended from F. Work at each prime
of F dividing g, trivialize the extended ideal by a generator in F,
and reduce modulo g. The finite etale quadratic algebra has a local
basis 1,theta with theta-conjugate(theta) a unit: this follows by
lifting a generator of the quadratic residue extension, or from the
two idempotents in the split case. For odd s the map x -> x+bar(x)
is the trace onto the base ring, which is surjective even in residue
characteristic two. For even s the map x -> x-bar(x) has image the
free rank-one summand generated by theta-bar(theta), by the unit
condition. In both cases its kernel and image have cardinality
|O_F/gO_F| locally, with the relevant local factor understood.
Multiply the local cardinalities. This argument also works for
prime-power g; it does not require division by two.

Use the literal rational trace pairing B(l,t)=Tr_E/Q(lt/g).
For any fractional ideal J, J^vee=g d^(-1)J^(-1), by the definition
of the codifferent. Hence

    L^vee ⊂ (gI)^vee = aa = g^(-1)d^(-nu-1),
    [aa:L^vee] = [L:gI] = g^f.

The second equality is ordinary lattice duality: the pairing between
the two finite quotient groups is perfect with values in Q/Z. This
proves the exact index rather than just the inclusion. For nonzero
l in aa, (l) ⊂ aa, so |N(l)| >= N(aa). Because the chosen complex
places are one from each conjugate pair,

    absl(l) = product_tau |tau(l)| = sqrt(|N(l)|),
    Nh = sqrt(N(aa)) = (g rd^(nu+1))^(-f).

Thus absl(l) >= Nh. In G4, nu=2r+2-2i and
e=-nu-1=2i-3-2r. For any positive c_tau the required identity is

    product_tau [c_tau (2pi/g)^e] / Nh
       = (product_tau c_tau) (2pi/rd)^(ef) g^((1-e)f)
       = (product_tau c_tau) (2pi/rd)^(ef) g^((m-2(i-1))f),

using m=2r+2. This also gives the same upper bound when Nh is
replaced by absl(l). There is no loss depending on the number of
places or the word. This verifies the arithmetic content of G4 under
the actual period-lattice and host pins.

As an independent normalization check, in ordinary complex Minkowski
coordinates with real Lebesgue measure, covol(O_E)=2^(-f)sqrt(|disc E|).
Consequently covol(L)=2^(-f)g^(3f)rd^(f(2nu+1)).
The product covol(L)covol(L^vee)=(g/2)^(2f), because the real
determinant of the trace pairing in these coordinates has absolute
value (2/g)^(2f). These statements are for this explicit measure;
the source uses its own coordinate change and Fourier convention,
so the actual kappaL is to be transported, not reset to this value.

## 2. Gluing the periodic positive kernels

Here is the global step of the G3 block argument in a form that also
works for non-principal lattices. Fix the finite list of heads, with
the same local determinant norms at each place as required in G3blk.
Let L_p denote the prescribed local additive period group; it is
compact open and equals O_{E,p} at almost all places. Suppose the
local block construction gives a positive semidefinite (PSD) matrix
B_p(c) for each residue c in E_p/L_p, with finite support, and

    0 <= A_p(t) <= B_p(t mod L_p),
    sum_c B_p(c) <= C_p.

Here A_p is the *weighted* local kernel of G3blk, including its
modK power, and C_p is the prescribed local previous-block kernel
including the determinant-norm factor. At unexceptional places assume
the actual spherical identity: B_p is supported on the zero residue,
B_p(0)=C_p=J (the all-ones matrix). The following gluing uses these
hypotheses exactly.

The map E -> direct_sum_p E_p/L_p is onto and has kernel L.
For a finitely supported tuple choose a rational integer denominator
D so that all D times the specified representatives are integral
locally. Include every place dividing D and every exceptional place.
At each such place choose a sufficiently deep integral congruence
subgroup contained in D L_p; the ordinary ideal Chinese remainder
theorem produces one numerator in O_E with all the prescribed
congruences. Dividing by D gives the desired rational element, also
integral at the other places. This proves surjectivity even when L_p
is only an O_F-module.

Use the entrywise (Schur) product and put

    Th(t) = Schur_product_p B_p(t mod L_p).

It is L-periodic, PSD, supported on finitely many global residue
classes and majorizes the actual product of the A_p(t). Indeed
the Schur product theorem and

    C ⊙ D - A ⊙ B = (C-A) ⊙ D + A ⊙ (D-B)

give monotonicity on PSD pairs. The infinite notation is harmless:
the unit J removes all unexceptional factors, and for every fixed
rational t all its denominators lie in finitely many places.
By the proved residue bijection, distributivity of the finite
Schur products gives

    sum_(t mod L) Th(t)
      = Schur_product_p (sum_c B_p(c))
      <= Schur_product_p C_p.

This is precisely the required form of the global block majorant
with the original determinant factors retained. There is no
additional class-number factor and no cost depending on the
number of heads. The inverse-difference support condition of G3blk,
its terminal equality t=t', and the actual local PSD majorants
(including their spherical identities) must still be supplied;
the gluing does not create them. Likewise G2's exact class-weight
counting needs CNT and the actual coefficient and density pins.

## 3. Exact Poisson on the resulting additive lattice

Let Lambda be a full real lattice in C^f, with actual covolume
kappa for the measure being used, and let Lambda^* be its
character-dual in the chosen Fourier convention. Suppose a
continuous nonnegative phi satisfies

    phi(t) <= C product_v (1+|t_v|)^(-3),

and the sums of |hat phi(sigma)| over all finite subsets of
Lambda^* minus zero are bounded by B. Then

    kappa sum_(z in S) phi(t0+z) <= |integral phi| + B

for every finite S ⊂ Lambda and every t0.

For completeness, the periodization P(t)=sum_Lambda phi(t+z)
converges uniformly on a compact fundamental parallelepiped:
the number of lattice points in any translated unit cube is
bounded uniformly by discreteness, and the product of the
two-dimensional decay sums with exponent 3 converges. Its Fourier
coefficients are hat phi(sigma)/kappa, by integration and tiling.
The finite-subset hypothesis gives absolute convergence of the
dual series. Its sum is continuous with the same Fourier
coefficients as P, hence equals P by uniqueness of Fourier series
on the compact torus (or Fejer approximation). Evaluate at t0
and use nonnegativity to pass from the full sum to S.
This proves PoissonPi of BlockEstimateWords for the exact measure
and character dual, without changing its constant or demanding
extra differentiability of phi.

A finite residue mask can be incorporated without another
estimate: applying the identity on each of its cosets and summing
multiplies each dual coefficient by the finite sum of its
characters. The zero-frequency multiplier is the exact number
of cosets; in particular it is not an adjustable normalization.

## 4. The additive global theta interface

This part is common to W06 and W08. For a number field K and a
finite-dimensional K-vector space with a nondegenerate bilinear
pairing, use a global additive character trivial on K and its
self-dual local measures. A finite Schwartz test is a finite
linear combination of indicators of cosets of a single compact
open additive subgroup U_f; compact support and local constancy
give such a common subgroup. The rational points in any such
coset are, by the same Chinese remainder argument, an affine
translate of the full archimedean lattice U_f intersect K^n.

The diagonal rational subgroup has covolume one for the global
self-dual measure: for the standard trace pairing this is the
different formula (the archimedean covolume sqrt(|disc K|) in
self-dual measure, times the finite product |disc K|^(-1/2)).
For a general lattice U_f the finite index and archimedean index
cancel. Changing the pairing by an invertible rational matrix
preserves the global normalization by the product formula.

Archimedean Poisson on each affine lattice now proves adelic
Poisson for these tests. On the finite side the exact Fourier
transform of a coset indicator is vol(U_f) times its character
phase times the indicator of U_f^perp. The phase at rational
dual points cancels/matches the archimedean shift by global
triviality on rational arguments. This recovers the same global
Fourier measure, not an arbitrary scalar per row. Finite linearity
proves the result for all factorizable Schwartz tests and their
finite sums.

Also the rational theta sum of a Schwartz test converges absolutely
and uniformly for compact families with a common finite support
bound and uniform archimedean Schwartz seminorms. This is the
same affine-lattice estimate. Thus a finite common Hecke
convolution commutes with rational theta summation, or a compactly
supported continuous convolution does so whenever the stated
uniform bounds on its translates hold, by dominated convergence.
This supplies the additive/finite-convolution mechanism once the
actual common Weil realization and its test vectors are fixed.
It does not prove the unitary splitting, the geometric AllTrue
package, or the cuspidal OntoAll statement, nor determine their
absolute see-saw scalar.
