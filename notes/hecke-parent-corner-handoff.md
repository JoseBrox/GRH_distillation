# Shared reduction: delete the residual negative corner before the rank-three count

24 November 2026. **Handoff from Hecke research to the RH/Dirichlet
research chat.** This is a proved simplification of the actual marked
rank-three integral deformation model. It applies to the already
reconstructed RH high-dyadic frame under an explicit stronger threshold
(and at 3 under its existing Xpp theorem), and to general Dirichlet
frames when the corresponding residual corner vanishes. It does not
supply the still-open rank-four or full general-Dirichlet automorphy
inputs. No full proof-map node is closed by this simplification.

See [the cubic version](../hecke/notes/cubic-corner-elimination.md)
and the original [inertial frame](../paper/shared/rh-dyadic-host-frame.tex),
[universal ring](../paper/shared/rh-dyadic-host-universal.tex), and
[integral count](../paper/shared/rh-dyadic-host-count.tex).

## 1. General marked rank-three lemma

Let O be a complete odd-residue-characteristic DVR with residue field
k. Work with an actual normalized polarized ordinary ring R as in
the parent DC3--DC7 construction: the multiplier-one group has
residual diagonal (Psi,1,Psi^(-1)), the torus weights are (1,0,-1),
the action of the normalized complex conjugation on its Lie algebra
is negative reversing transpose, and both adjacent entries of the
marked difference r(x)-r(cx^(-1)c) are zero. Its diagonal is
d diag(1,0,-1) with d a residual unit. The normalized ordinary
local conditions at the coefficient prime are the specified ordered
flag with fixed inertia characters; away from that prime use the
actual prescribed inertia-kernel condition.

Let H^1_L(-2) denote the residual negative-corner Selmer space,
with the exact local coboundary condition at the coefficient
prime and the prescribed inertia-kernel conditions elsewhere.
Assume H^1_L(-2)=0 (the original Xpp condition in this model).

Then the relative tangent of R has no weight-minus-two component.
Indeed such a tangent is an actual cocycle in k E_31 satisfying
these local conditions. It is a coboundary by the assumption.
Its primitive in k E_31 must be fixed by complex conjugation
because the normalization at c is fixed. On that corner line
conjugation acts as minus one, so the primitive is zero.
Thus the normalized tangent itself is zero, not just its class.

In the actual homogeneous minimal presentation of R by entry
displacements, there is no weight-minus-two variable. This is
complete Nakayama applied to the relative cotangent, not a
rationalization or removal of a nonreduced component. All
negative-weight elements are in the ideal generated by the
actual weight-minus-one adjacent lower entry displacements:
every homogeneous monomial of negative degree has such a
factor. This is also true in the finite-sum graded algebra
using the homogeneous formal-series presentation, by collecting
monomials divisible by each of the finitely many generators.

A two-sided point of the parent marked ring is type I
(absolutely irreducible on the multiplier-one subgroup) or
type II (the irreducible outer two-by-two block and separate
middle line). The coordinate projectors of the marked
difference give this classification in either characteristic:
the two adjacent arrows of each sign occur together by
polarization, and any adjacent pair plus an opposite sign
makes the graph strongly connected. In type II all adjacent
entries vanish. In particular all the actual negative
generators above vanish, so all negative-weight elements
vanish, contradicting two-sidedness.

**Conclusion.** Under Xpp there is no two-sided type-II point,
in characteristic zero or on a characteristic-prime DVR curve,
and likewise in the algebraized finite-sum graded point space.
All original relation counts retain their exact integral
meaning, with n_-2=0 and no defining equations of that weight
when the parent defect in weight -2 is zero.

In particular the parent generic length comparison
[abstract length](../paper/shared/selmer-core-wrapper.tex) can
use only type-I rigidity and the type-I trace spectrum in this
range. No Kato argument for the rank-two outer points is needed.
The finite-invariant-ring proof needs only to exclude the
irreducible characteristic-prime curves. This statement does
not assert that the *auxiliary variable-weight propagation
ring* has no type-E components: its local conditions are
different and it is not the fixed-inertia ring R.

## 2. Explicit RH range at all odd coefficient primes

In the actual RH inertial frame DH1 let psi=psi_2 psi_odd,
psi_odd^2=1, and psi_2 primitive of conductor 2^b. Assume
the stated host and local conditions, including ell not
dividing the host modulus. The residual negative corner
character is


```math
\gamma=\overline{\eta_4\varepsilon^{-2e}\psi^{-2}},
```


where eta_4 is the character of Q(i). This is odd. The
odd quadratic factors disappear on squaring. The primitive
odd conductor of a characteristic-zero finite-order lift
of gamma therefore divides ell (use the Teichmuller lift
of the residual cyclotomic character).
For b>=4 the dyadic part has exact conductor 2^(b-1):
squaring lowers the conductor of the primitive even-unit
component by one, and eta_4 has conductor four and cannot
cancel the resulting primitive character on 1+4.
Take d=ord_4(ell), h=v_2(ell^d-1). The odd conductor
character's order divides ell-1, hence divides ell^d-1.

The parent's proved
[Bernoulli-unit and cocycle theorem](../paper/shared/selmer-dyadic-twist-vanishing.tex)
applies when


```math
\ell\,4^h\le2^{b-1}.
 \tag{1}
```


For ell=1 mod 4, 2^h<=ell-1; for ell=3 mod 4,
2^h=2*2^{v_2(ell+1)}<=2(ell+1). In particular
the simple sufficient threshold


```math
2^b\ge8\ell(\ell+1)^2
 \tag{2}
```


implies (1) and DH1. To apply the exact cocycle corollary,
a negative-corner cocycle is zero on the original away
inertia kernels, of index prime to ell in inertia.
At ell its restriction is a local coboundary and so
vanishes on the kernel of gamma in inertia. All the
corollary's relative-index conditions are satisfied.
It follows that H^1_L(-2)=0, hence Section 1 applies.
At ell=3 the parent DH frame already proves the stronger
literal Xpp result under DH1, using its separate dyadic
Bernoulli-three calculation; no extra threshold (2) is
needed there.

This removes the type-II branch from the actual integral
count DC6--DC7 and its characteristic-zero tangent proof
in these ranges. The original full proof at all DH1 primes
remains valid and should be retained: for conductors
between DH1 and the new threshold the simpler branch
need not apply. The already reconstructed point suppliers
are not discarded.

## 3. General Dirichlet threshold for the same frame

Consider an admitted parent rank-three frame with
compensated character Psi=epsilon^e psi and multiplier
character eta of a fixed imaginary quadratic field.
Suppose its finite residual character is of prime-to-ell
order and its moving odd auxiliary factors are quadratic.
Let M_0 be a fixed multiple of the conductor of the
nonauxiliary odd part and the odd part of eta's conductor.
Let b be the exact dyadic conductor exponent of psi
and assume b sufficiently large that squaring lowers
it to b-1 and multiplying by the fixed dyadic part
of eta cannot change that primitive conductor
(for quadratic eta, whose dyadic conductor is at most
eight, b>=5 suffices).

The negative corner is again gamma=bar(eta Psi^(-2)).
Its primitive finite lift is odd, of odd conductor
f dividing M_0 ell, after squaring has removed
the moving quadratic factors. If d is any positive
integer with 4|ell^d-1 and the order of that odd
character dividing ell^d-1, put h=v_2(ell^d-1).
The exact sufficient criterion for Section 1 is


```math
f4^h\le2^{b-1}.
 \tag{3}
```


Such d exists because the character order is prime
to ell. If that order divides (ell-1)r, where r is
fixed prime to ell, one may take d to be any even
multiple of ord_r(ell) that also makes the power-sum
1+ell+...+ell^(d-1) divisible by r; for example
d=2r*ord_r(ell) works. This is bounded in terms of r
and so (3) has a sufficient threshold C_{M_0,r} ell(ell+1)^2
by the elementary lifting-the-exponent identity for
v_2(ell^d-1). For clarity, the condition that
r divides the power-sum matters when r and ell-1
are not coprime.

The original finite inertia-kernel and coefficient-prime
coboundary conditions must still hold, as in Section 2.
Where the actual source has the stronger Xpp already,
one simply uses that result. We do not assert that all
general-Dirichlet character data satisfy these admitted
frame hypotheses, that the ell-primary finite characters
can be ignored, or that a rank-four model has the same
negative-weight monoid.

## 4. A separate reusable strong-irreducibility criterion

For a prime-dimensional representation over a complete
DVR with residual semisimplification a sum of distinct
characters, if every character ratio has order greater
than n!, and no open subgroup acts by scalars, then
every irreducible representation remains irreducible
on open subgroups. The proof in
[the Hecke monodromy note](../hecke/notes/cubic-irreducible-monodromy.md)
uses Clifford theory and linear independence of the
residual characters on the permutation kernel. It
works in both characteristics; for a characteristic-prime
curve with finite determinant, scalar action on an open
subgroup would imply finite image, incompatible with
irreducibility and the split residual semisimplification.
For a characteristic-zero ordinary representation with
distinct infinite inertia weights, scalar action is
excluded by those weights.

This can simplify the corresponding imprimitive-image
parts of the parent rank-two and rank-three curve/point
arguments where the residual ratio orders meet the
criterion. It is not a substitute for all the N1--N4
conditions of their auxiliary Nice-curve propagation,
nor a rank-four irreducibility theorem.

**Handoff acceptance.** Use these as local proof
simplifications in the actual parent ring. Do not
mark a new full node closed, or use them to assert the
unresolved Case II / general-Dirichlet point suppliers.
The main new Hecke automorphy problem is different:
its irreducible high-dyadic points have connected
monodromy SL3 and cannot be constructed by the
original symmetric-square seed.

## Follow-up: both negative weights

The [two-negative-weight theorem](../hecke/notes/cubic-two-negative-weight-elimination.md), §1, proves an integral strengthening of the above shared lemma. If the actual strict residual adjacent and corner groups both vanish, the marked tangent has neither negative weight, the entire normalized ring is upper triangular, and its full weight-zero ring is the coefficient DVR when the multiplier-one base has class number one. This discharges both irreducible point premises without any automorphy assertion. It applies to the Gaussian parent frame exactly as to the Eisenstein one; verify the residual adjacent vanishing before use. The [finite class-rank criterion](../hecke/notes/cubic-finite-class-rank-certificates.md) also gives a general finite-layer Iwasawa test via abelianization: for a totally ramified cyclotomic tower above the coefficient prime, the kernel of the coinvariant-to-class-group map is generated by at most the number of primes above that prime minus one. The same proof applies to the strict split class group by killing decomposition groups in the split tower. This may replace asymptotic finite-generation verification for an individual fixed field, but does not by itself control a moving family on common windows. No changes to the parent rank-four count are claimed.

**Parent disposition (24 November).** Incorporated as [the integral corner reduction](../paper/shared/rank-three-corner-elimination.tex) in both manuscripts. The threshold and local hypotheses are retained. The full intermediate-conductor and rank-four arguments remain in place; no change is made to the RH weighted bound or the general Dirichlet W04 status.
