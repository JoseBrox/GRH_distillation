# Auxiliary CM primes and cofinal tied Selmer selection

26 November 2026. **Proved relative to classical class field
theory and Chebotarev:** the precise auxiliary-prime supply of
the [classical-duality descent](lsernau-classical-tied-descent.md),
with the source's character conditions. Together the two notes
give the tied-length and bounded-cardinality theorem on the
stated Selmer data. The remaining work is application to the
moving arithmetic data and simultaneous Stickelberger systems;
this is not the completed projection-denominator theorem.

Source: [Sernau–Anthropic 72fead1][src], based on Alpöge and
Claude. The construction below distils [AuxiliaryPrimes][aux],
220–395, [Mirror][mirror], 131–230, and [Descent][descent],
247–290. In particular it retains the idele-character witness,
not merely existence of primes with a suitable norm.

## 1. A single general construction

Let E be CM, with complex conjugation c acting on the chosen
algebraic closure of Q, and G=Gal(Ebar/E). Let q=p^N, N≥1,
where p is prime. Write S for a finite set of protected finite
places, S^c for its conjugates, and R for a finite set of
places to avoid. Let B be S∪S^c together with the p-places.
Assume z∈G is in the normal subgroup generated by inertia
at R\B. Given any open neighborhood W of z, we claim there
are a degree-one prime P of E with P≠P^c and

    P∉R∪R^c∪B,       NP≡1 mod q,

a choice of place above P, a tame inertia generator delta_P,
an arithmetic Frobenius lift phi_P∈W, and a continuous character
chi:G→Z/q with the following properties:

    chi(delta_P)=1,         chi(phi_P)=0,
    chi(g^c)=-chi(g).

Moreover chi is unramified outside P,P^c and trivial on the
entire decomposition groups at B. There is an idele-class
character theta of the finite ideles, killed on principal
ideles, such that

    chi(Art(x)) = theta(x/c(x)),

and theta kills ideles that are units off B and congruent to
1 at P. The last assertion is the source's IsFromU1
condition for any S'⊂S∪{places above p}; it is stronger
than merely saying the conductor divides PP^c.

**Proof.** Enlarge B to a finite c-stable set T with the
following two properties:

1. T is disjoint from (R∪R^c)\B.
2. The ideal classes of primes in T generate Cl(E).

Such a T exists without a prime-distribution argument.
For each of the finitely many ideal classes choose a
fractional ideal representative supported outside the
forbidden finite set: multiply any representative by a
principal fractional ideal with the required valuations
there, by weak approximation. Take the union of its
prime factors over the classes, and also their conjugates.
The forbidden set is c-stable. Thus the finite ideles are
the product of E^× and the group I_T of ideles that are
units off T.

The S-unit theorem gives finitely many generators u_j
of O_{E,T}^×. The Kummer extension

    K = E(mu_q, all u_j^{1/q})

is finite Galois over E and unramified off T: at a place
not in T the u_j are units and q is prime to residue
characteristic, so every q-th root of u_j and every
q-th root of unity lies in a local unramified extension
(reduce the polynomial and use Hensel's lemma in a
sufficiently large finite residue field). Therefore the
normal closure of the inertias at R\B acts trivially
on K, since those places are not in T. In particular z
fixes K.

Choose a finite extension L/Q Galois over Q, containing
E and K, large enough that the coset of Gal(Ebar/L)
represented by z is contained in W. This is possible
since G has its profinite topology. Chebotarev over Q
gives infinitely many rational primes r unramified in L
with Frobenius conjugate to z|L. Choose a place of L
over r with Frobenius exactly z|L. Its restriction to E
has residue degree one: the decomposition group of this
unramified place in L/Q is generated by z|L, which fixes E.
Choose r away from the finitely many rational primes under
T, R, R^c and those ramified in E/E^+. Since the resulting
P of E has degree one and is unramified in E/E^+, it is
not fixed by c (an inert prime has relative residue degree
two). NP=r, so P has prime norm. Extend the chosen place
to the algebraic closure. Every arithmetic Frobenius lift
at that place restricts to z|L, so lies in W and fixes K.

In particular q divides NP-1 and every T-unit is a
q-th power in the residue field at P. Since the residue
multiplicative group is cyclic, take a surjective
homomorphism

    log_P : k(P)^× → Z/q.

Extend it to E_P^× by giving one uniformizer value zero
and using reduction on units. Because P∉T, log_P
kills every T-unit. On I_T the continuous homomorphism
x↦log_P(x_P) is thus zero on I_T∩E^×. By the product
decomposition above it descends uniquely to a continuous
character theta of the finite idele classes. Continuity
follows since I_T is open in the finite ideles. In
particular theta kills every full local factor E_w^×
for w∈T, and at w∉T∪{P} it kills local units.

The character x↦theta(x/c(x)) is zero on E^× and
is anticyclotomic. Global class field theory, extending
the finite-idele character trivially on the connected
archimedean factors of this totally imaginary field,
identifies it with a continuous finite Galois character
chi. The restrictions of theta just listed prove the
ramification and decomposition assertions for chi.
On inertia at P, the conjugate factor is trivial because
P^c≠P and the conjugate units have component 1 at P.
Local reciprocity identifies the tame p-primary quotient
of inertia with the p-primary quotient of the cyclic
residue multiplicative group. The image of a chosen
tame generator in Z/q is therefore a generator.
Rescale log_P and theta by a unit of Z/q so that
chi(delta_P)=1, including if the source uses a
different generator or reciprocity convention.

Finally, if phi is any chosen arithmetic Frobenius
lift, set

    phi_P = phi delta_P^j,    j≡-chi(phi) mod q.

This is still arithmetic Frobenius at the same place.
Since L/Q is unramified there, delta_P acts trivially
on L; thus phi_P has precisely the required restriction
and stays in W. It now satisfies chi(phi_P)=0.
All the asserted properties follow. QED.

The argument explains why it is important to require
a neighborhood of an inertial product, not an arbitrary
Frobenius prescribed independently of the protected
units. No effective Chebotarev estimate or GRH is
needed. We deliberately do not claim a quantitative
bound for the norms of these primes.

## 2. Exact tied-prime application

Use the data of the classical-duality descent note,
with all S_i contained in a fixed finite S_0. As in
the source, the prescribed g_0 for the first prime
is inertial at a place of S_0 away from p. Fix N>M
and the desired neighborhood of g_0 (including the
finite-level matrix comparison to g_0 and its
conjugate inverse used to release the first ties).

For the singleton set take S empty in §1 and let R
include all places over rational primes lying below
S_0, as well as g_0's place. The normal-inertia
hypothesis holds, and the selected first prime v_0
and its conjugate avoid these places. Give all data
zero slope at v_0. This is a source IsTiedPrimes
configuration. The norm of v_0 is prime and no
original place divides this rational prime.

Suppose J is already a tied configuration, let i
be a datum, and h∈H_i be any commutator-group element
as defined in the descent note. Put

    S=J\{v_0},       z=delta_{v_0} h.

Let R contain S_0∪S_0^c, J∪J^c, the finite set to
avoid in the mirror argument, and all places above
the rational prime Nv_0. Both gamma_i and gamma_i^c
are inertial away from p at original places, so
every commutator with either of them lies in the
normal subgroup generated by inertia at R\B.
The first tame generator lies in the same subgroup.
Thus z meets the hypothesis of §1.

Shrink the desired neighborhood of z by the
following finitely many open conditions:

* g and g^c act trivially on W_e for datum i and
  on mu_q;
* for all old characters chi_v, their values on
  g are chi_v(z).

The target z satisfies the first by unramifiedness
of the datum at v_0 and its conjugate and by
scalar action of gamma_i,gamma_i^c (all their
commutators act trivially). It satisfies the
second by definition. More explicitly, the old
characters kill h; the first character takes
value 1 at delta_{v_0}, and every later character
is unramified at v_0. Consequently

    chi_v(z) = 1 if v=v_0, and 0 otherwise.

Apply §1 in this smaller neighborhood. The new
prime P has all the required avoidance, degree-one
and split-pair properties; it avoids all places
above Nv_0. Its own character is trivial on its
own chosen Frobenius and at the decomposition
groups of every later old prime v∈J\{v_0} and its
conjugate. This gives the new column of old-prime
character values, without needing a zero value at
the original first Frobenius. The smaller
neighborhood gives the new row of character
values under every old character and ensures both
new Frobenius matrices for datum i are the
identity mod p^M. The character of P also
satisfies IsFromU1 with protected set consisting
of the later old primes and the p-places.

Therefore P is admissible as a new auxiliary
prime for datum i and may be appended with any
slope for i and zero for all others. Every
condition of IsTiedPrimes and the new-prime
requirements used in Mirror 175–230 is preserved.
The choice works for every h, every neighborhood,
and every finite exclusion set, as needed for
paired-prime reciprocity.

## 3. Consequence and exact limits

This supplies both hypotheses of the
[classical-duality descent](lsernau-classical-tied-descent.md).
For fixed source Selmer data and M,O satisfying
the residue-field size condition, and for every
N>M, it follows that there exists a tied
configuration J with

    |J| ≤ 1 + sum_{i,k=1}^e len C_{i,k}(empty),
    len C_{i,e}(J) ≤ a_i+b_i.

The upper bound on cardinality is independent of
N. The bounds a_i,b_i are the source's global
conjugate-dual and local defect terms, not
arbitrary upper bounds on the original untwisted
Selmer groups. Thus the Selmer portion of the
selected-twist construction now has ordinary
proofs from the actual datum hypotheses:
classical prime supply, finite duality,
cofinal tied-length descent, finite polynomial
presentations and small witnesses, and
simultaneous torsion specialization. The last
two are in [finite presentations][finite] and
[twist specialization][spec].

This does not yet prove the simultaneous
Stickelberger polynomial witnesses, the
construction of the source's actual data at
all coefficient primes, their defect estimates,
or the integral projection comparison. In
particular it must not be counted as completion
of W04 or any whole legacy proof-map node.
The proof is valid for general CM E under the
listed hypotheses and can be reused by the
Hecke track with its own datum checks.
