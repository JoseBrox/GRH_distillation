# Protected quadratic base-change steps

This proves the precise arithmetic existence input in
[odd-rank patching](lsernau-odd-rank-patching.md), not the
automorphic base-change or split-case attachment theorem.
It uses Dirichlet's theorem in arithmetic progressions, the
two-squares theorem, Hensel's lemma, the Chinese remainder
theorem and global class-field reciprocity. No Grunwald
existence assertion with unspecified local restrictions is needed.

Source: [QuadraticStep.lean](https://github.com/lsernau/grh-lean/blob/72fead102f663d5c7a407964a13dc0cf9d977207/grh/src/Lib/GaloisRep/Main/QuadraticStep.lean),
lines 134--181, 241--281, 353--541, and
[Grunwald.lean](https://github.com/lsernau/grh-lean/blob/72fead102f663d5c7a407964a13dc0cf9d977207/grh/src/Lib/ClassField/Grunwald.lean),

## 1. Precise protected-step statement

Let \(E\) be CM containing the 5040th roots of unity,
\(c\) complex conjugation, and \(n\ge1\) an integer.
Let \(\Gamma=G_E\). We construct a quadratic character
\(\chi:\Gamma\to\frac12\mathbb Z/\mathbb Z\) and put
\(\chi^c(g)=\chi(cgc^{-1})\),
\(U=\ker(\chi+\chi^c)\),
\(N=U\cap\ker\chi\).
The fixed field \(E'\) of \(U\) is a quadratic CM extension
of \(E\), contains an imaginary quadratic subfield
\(\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-D})\) for \(D\equiv3\bmod4\), and satisfies:

* Every decomposition group over \(n\) is contained in \(U\);
  the inertia groups over \(n\) lie in \(N\).
  At a split place of \(E\) over \(n\), the whole decomposition
  group lies in \(N\).
* Every place of \(E'\) over \(n\) lies above a split
  prime of \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-D})\) away from \(D\).
* There is a finite norm cutoff \(m\) such that the subgroup
  generated by inertia groups over places prime to \(n\) of
  norm at most \(m\) surjects onto \(\Gamma/N\).
* The extension \(E'/E\), together with the Hecke character
  \(\exp(2\pi i\chi\circ\operatorname{Art}_E)\), has the
  source's step conditions, including an integral element of
  trace \(1\) and its exact symmetrized norm criterion.

Here all decomposition and inertia groups can be taken at any
extensions of the finite places to an algebraic closure.

## 2. A norm with the required congruence and a fresh valuation

Choose a rational positive integer \(R\) divisible by \(4n\)
and divisible by \((2n)^k\) for \(k\) sufficiently large that
\(k>2v_w(2)\) at every \(w\mid2n\). It suffices, for example,
to take \(k=N_{E/\mathbb Q}(4)\). Then
\(v_w(R)>2v_w(2)\) at these places.
Write \(b\) for the odd part of \(R\).

Since \(i\in E\) and \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-7})\subset E\),
we have an integer \(\omega=(1+\sqrt{-7})/2\) of trace \(1\)
and norm \(2\) in the latter subfield. Its inclusion follows
also directly from the quadratic Gauss sum in
\(\mathbb Q(\zeta_7)\). Consequently
\[
 C=(1+2b\omega)(1+2b\bar\omega)
   =1+2b+8b^2\equiv3\pmod4,\qquad (C,R)=1 .
\]
By Dirichlet choose a rational prime \(q\) with
\(qC\equiv-1\bmod R\) avoiding the primes dividing \(Cn\)
and the discriminant of \(E\). Since \(q\equiv1\bmod4\),
write \(q=s^2+t^2\) with integers \(s,t\). Set
\[
 D=Cq,\qquad \alpha=(1+2b\omega)(s+it).
\]
Thus \(\alpha\bar\alpha=D\), \(D\equiv-1\bmod R\),
and at every \(P\mid q\), \(v_P(D)=1\) because \(q\)
is unramified in \(E\). These places are prime to \(n\).
In particular \(D>0\) and \(-D\) is not a square in \(E\).
The congruence and norm have been imposed simultaneously.

## 3. The local twist and its two characters

At each pair \(w,\bar w\) of distinct places dividing \(n\),
prescribe residues of an integer \(z\) as \(1,\alpha\),
respectively, modulo \(w^k,\bar w^k\).
At each fixed place \(w=\bar w\) dividing \(n\), prescribe
\(z\equiv1\bmod w^k\). Chinese remaindering gives a nonzero
\(z\in\mathcal O_E\) (if \(n=1\) use \(z=1\)).
Put \(T=z\bar z\in\mathcal O_{E^+}\setminus\{0\}\).
The factors \(\alpha,\bar\alpha\) are units at all \(w\mid n\)
since \(D\equiv-1\bmod R\).
At the first place of a split pair, \(\alpha T\equiv D\equiv-1
\bmod w^k\), and at its partner \(\alpha T\equiv\alpha^2
\bmod\bar w^k\). Both are squares in their local fields:
in the former case \(-1=i^2\), in the latter the indicated
square is a unit; the ratio to that square differs from 1
by valuation \(>2v_w(2)\). Hensel applies to \(X^2\) minus
that ratio at \(1\).

At a fixed place above \(n\), \(\alpha T\) is a unit.
Such a place is not dyadic: in \(\mathbb Q(\zeta_7)\),
the decomposition subgroup at 2 is generated by 2
modulo 7, of order 3, hence does not contain \(-1\).
If a prime of \(E\) over 2 were fixed by conjugation,
its restriction to this cyclotomic subfield would be fixed,
a contradiction. Thus the quadratic extension obtained by
adjoining \(\sqrt{\alpha T}\) at a fixed place is unramified
or trivial, by the unit-square test in odd residual
characteristic.

Take \(a=\sqrt{\alpha T}\) in the chosen algebraic closure
and let \(\chi\) be its sign character, written additively
with values \(0,1/2\). Its conjugate is the character of
\(c(a)\). Since
\[
      y=i\,a\,c(a)/T,\qquad y^2=-D ,
\]
the character \(\chi+\chi^c\) is precisely the sign of \(y\).
So \(E'=E(y)\) is quadratic over \(E\).
It is CM: since \(i\in E\), it equals \(E(\sqrt D)\),
a totally imaginary quadratic extension of \(E^+(\sqrt D)\),
which is totally real. It inherits the roots of unity and
has the required imaginary quadratic subfield.

At every \(w\mid 2n\) the congruence
\(-D\equiv1\bmod w^k\) and Hensel show that \(y\) is already
in \(E_w\). Thus the decomposition groups lie in \(U\)
and \(E'/E\) is split there. At \(w\mid n\) the local
analysis of \(\alpha T\) shows inertia kills \(\chi\);
at distinct conjugate places the entire decomposition
group kills \(\chi\). This is precisely the stated
containment in \(N\).

For every rational prime \(p\mid n\) the polynomial
\(X^2-X+(D+1)/4\) reduces to \(X(X-1)\) modulo \(p\),
with two distinct roots even at \(p=2\).
Its discriminant is \(-D\), prime to \(p\).
Hence \(p\) splits in \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt{-D})\), away
from \(D\), also if \(D\) was not squarefree. In
particular the source's two coprime elements
\((1+\sqrt{-D})/2\) and its conjugate have the indicated
membership at the two places.

## 4. Generation by fresh inertia and the exact step criterion

The character of \(y\) is nontrivial on inertia at
\(P\mid q\): adjoining a square root of an element of
odd valuation is ramified of degree two. Choose
\(h\in I_P\) with \((\chi+\chi^c)(h)=1/2\).
The map
\[
 \Gamma/N\longrightarrow(\mathbb Z/2)^2,\qquad
 g\longmapsto(2\chi(g),2\chi^c(g))
\]
uses the identification of \(\frac12\mathbb Z/\mathbb Z\)
with \(\mathbb Z/2\), and is injective. The images of
\(h\) and \(chc^{-1}\) are \((1,0),(0,1)\) in some order.
They generate the target, so the map is an isomorphism.
Both inertia elements are at places prime to \(n\) of
norm \(N(P)=N(\bar P)\). Taking \(m=N(P)\) proves the
generation clause for every subgroup containing all the
specified inertia groups. This is generation modulo \(N\),
not merely modulo \(U\).

The trace on integers of \(E'/E\) is onto:
at primes away from 2 the trace of \(1\) is a unit,
and at dyadic places the extension is locally split
and trace on the product of integer rings is addition.
Since the trace image is an ideal and localizes to
the unit ideal everywhere, it contains \(1\).

Write \(\mu=\exp(2\pi i\chi\circ\operatorname{Art}_E)\)
on the finite ideles, with the Artin reciprocity convention
used in the source. It has open kernel and is trivial on
principal ideles: \(E\) is totally imaginary and the
infinite ideles are connected. Equivariance of the Artin
map under \(c\) gives
\[
 \mu(z\bar z)=\exp(2\pi i(\chi+\chi^c)
                                   (\operatorname{Art}_E z)).
\]
By the norm theorem of global class-field theory this is
\(1\) exactly on \(E^\times
N_{E'/E}(\mathbb A_{E',f}^\times)\). Thus this is the
actual step character, not an unrelated quadratic twist.
Together with the degree, CM property and trace computation,
this verifies the source's step conditions.

For the increasing protected sets in odd-rank patching,
apply the construction to any rational integer divisible
by the residue characteristics of all places to be
protected, including \(\ell\). One may enlarge subsequent
integers to protect every place below the inertia cutoff
of the initial step. Therefore the arithmetic protected
steps required in the patching proof now exist.

The outstanding inputs are the weak automorphic base change,
split-case Galois attachment and its local comparison, the
labelled Iwahori/Shintani transfer, and the nonzero theta
lifts needed in the two-string argument. This chapter does
not deduce these from the field construction.
