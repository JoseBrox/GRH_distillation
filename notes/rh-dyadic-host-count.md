# The actual inertial integral count at three

**Classification: closed supplier obligation.** [Full proof](../paper/shared/rh-dyadic-host-count.tex). This closes the ordinary RH large-conductor Case V at 3 and the entire exponent-one residual product, and also bounds every higher-twist character at 3 of dyadic conductor >72. It does not close W04 or a whole proof-map node.

## The proved statement

For the original high-dyadic-conductor RH inertial frame, with its original universal ring and the original residual condition Xpp, there is a finite family of pairwise nonisomorphic type-I representations such that

    length_O Sha_O(a) <= V <= 2 e_O sum_i v_ell(tr r_i(phi) - tr r_0(phi))

for a single integer V and every nonzero a in O. The source's original normalized strict length consequently has a uniform polynomial bound in ell N e. At 3 the previous dyadic Bernoulli theorem supplies Xpp at every e. The source threshold 9216 at e=1 is above 72, and the nonresonant bound covers all primes >=5. Hence the logarithm of the entire exponent-one residual product is bounded by (2N)^B, with the original weights, for an absolute B.

## Why the integral comparison is valid

The proof uses the literal inertial deformation functor, not the different good-data ordinary component. Local lifts at the coefficient prime use the Hensel ordered flag, the procyclic diagonal lift, and vanishing of positive-weight second cohomology. The exact relative obstruction spaces bound homogeneous minimal relations by their dimensions. Padding with zeros gives counts n_-2, n_-1+1, n_0, n_1-1, n_2-1. The finite sum of homogeneous series modulo those actual relations is the literal source graded algebra, including coefficient torsion.

The nonpositive cotangent span follows from VanE and the two marks. A fixed point is the augmentation by Cl1. A reducible two-sided point would have an isolated nonzero lower corner; dividing it by a minimum-valuation entry yields exactly the residual weight -2 cocycle, which Xpp excludes. The type-I points and all dual-number tangents use the previous integral evaluation and automorphy theorem. Rigidity and the two marks leave only the torus tangent. The actual trace element is nonzero there.

The established abstract length theorem now applies. Independently, the normalized cocycle of every original Sha_O(a) class gives an injection into the dual of the same integral negative cotangent quotient modulo a, proving the simultaneous bound without saturating that quotient. Distinct torus orbits give pairwise nonisomorphic representations on the multiplier-one group by the two marks and Schur's lemma.
