# The actual RH inertial universal ring: finiteness and automorphy

**Classification: closed finite-module and automorphy inputs to the
original inertial count, using its arbitrary Good predicate.**
[Complete proof](../paper/shared/rh-dyadic-host-universal.tex).

This is a direct application of the established auxiliary frame, finite-point
and characteristic-prime triangularity theorems to the actual inertial
universal ring. The original local conjugator survives every complete DVR
test. Its pullback satisfies the earlier OrdPt hypotheses, with the same
marks and the original character. The actual quotient therefore satisfies
the characteristic-prime curve criterion. This proves finiteness of its
literal functional weight-zero module, including its nilpotents and torsion.

Every point of the actual graded algebra can be moved by the permitted
torus into an integral characteristic-zero point. The proof uses the
valuation inequalities for balanced monomials and the finite generation
of each weight space over the weight-zero ring. The finite point and
ordinary classicality theorems give automorphy over the actual auxiliary
abelian CM field. Soluble CM descent (Thorne, Lemma 2.7(2)) gives automorphy
over the Gaussian field with the prescribed twist. Strong irreducibility
is retained. The previously proved crystalline/trace/rigidity theorem
uses only these properties.

Importantly, the stronger source predicate BPrime_s contains WitA,
which includes nonresonance of all powers of certain local ratios.
We do not claim it follows simply from residual separation. The original
AbstractCount theorem permits an arbitrary predicate Good; the weaker
predicate just proved suffices for its arithmetic point and rigidity
hypotheses. The actual universal ring, local equations and Selmer
lengths are unchanged.

The outstanding step at 3 is the exact integral graded-algebra count
and its presentation/length comparison. No completed proof-map node,
full integral upper estimate or Lean rebuild is claimed.
