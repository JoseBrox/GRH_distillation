# The two original rank-four links and potential-modularity image ideal

**Status:** the two links, original determinant maps and their original sum ideal are constructed, using the established ordinary-level and ordinary-family suppliers. The ideal satisfies the finite-weight and seed-local-zero clauses; at the remaining RH arithmetic weights e>=2 it satisfies the original weak purity clause and yields cuspidal attachment over L for irreducible points. This is not yet the full PMExAlt theorem.

See [the shared proof](../paper/shared/rh-rank-four-link-ideal.tex), following the [seed support proof](rh-rank-four-seed-support.md).

The common character is built by finite ray class duality and the rank-one anticyclotomic quotient of the ray class tower of the original imaginary quadratic field K. A principal-unit square-root twist and the parity correction give the precise cyclotomic multiplier and integral local weights. The two distinct rational distinguished primes are chosen by Chebotarev using the source's condition that the residual cyclotomic character is not generated by the root characters. The fields are unchanged.

Integral Amitsur's formula supplies the original determinant maps, including in coefficient characteristic 3. The original killing, residue, polarization, ordered flag and local torsion conditions are retained. The same sum of the image ideals is used for every clause.

The finite-weight argument reduces modulo a prime in the weight-zero locus. The ordinary weight specialization is the residue evaluation, so its ordinary Hecke coefficients are integral over the finite residue field. Squares of matrices on the quadratic coset supply the remaining coefficients by an integral monic polynomial identity. The universal coefficient-generation property forces the quotient to be the residue field. The seed theorem then gives local zero on every prime below every original seed. For e>=2, the arithmetic-point decomposition of the actual ordinary Hecke quotient has no nontrivial Speh length: such a length would give two residual diagonal characters differing by the residual cyclotomic character, contrary to Ok.g2. The old ordinary-family purity and classicality clauses apply without invoking ModUAlt.
