Its period factors are unit multiples of \(t^{Q(y)}\), with \(Q(0)=0\) and \(Q(y)>0\) for every nonzero period index. 5. Choose the theta generator by this zero coefficient and a constant Raynaud Lie basis. 7. An original integral eigenframe is an integral matrix U in a full Lie basis. Its key coefficient is \((\det U)^4u\), with u a unit. The possibly nonunit determinant is retained with its correct positive exponent. No integral freeness of the ramified coefficient eigenspace decomposition is used.

## Identified obstruction: arbitrary principal lines

The boundary-unit assertion must not be generalized to all symmetric principal lines. On an elliptic curve, for the odd symmetric divisor at the identity, adjunction gives

\[
\mathrm{TH}=e^*\mathcal O(0)=\operatorname{Lie}G=\omega^{-1},
\qquad \Delta^4=\omega^{12}.
\]

The corresponding integral key is the discriminant section up to a universal unit; on the Tate family its product expansion is \(q\prod_{n\ge1}(1-q^n)^{24}\), of boundary order one. Its key coefficient is therefore not a boundary unit in an extending Hodge frame. Equivalently its toric half-character is shifted, and the unnormalized odd theta function has a fractional q-leading power.

The actual primitive line used in this project has the stronger zero-coefficient and strict quadratic-positivity properties above. They exclude that toric shift and are used explicitly. The counterexample explains why good reduction plus constant analytic norm would not suffice by themselves.
