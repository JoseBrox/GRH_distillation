# The active determinant manuscript

The [RH entry](../rh/main.tex) and [Dirichlet entry](../grh/main.tex) share [one mathematical body](body.tex). The argument is presented in order: exact prime remainder and zero criterion; common arithmetic data; short forms and spanning; selected twists and long forms; the corrected determinant inequality; the analytic conclusion. The proofs of specialist inputs are linked at their use, not appended in chronological order.

The [guide](../guide/main.tex) explains the idea; the [detailed blueprint](../../blueprint/merged-proof-plan.md) specifies the interfaces. No complete conventional proof is claimed. The old manuscripts are [historical alternatives](../archive/README.md), not extra premises.
