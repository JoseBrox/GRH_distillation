The project reconstructs the proof of Alp\"oge with Claude through
the \href{\sourcepin}{Sernau--Anthropic rewriting}. It is not yet a
completed ordinary proof. The underlying idea is to compare how large
one determinant is at the ordinary and the prime-adic places.

\section{Three estimates, one space}
Arithmetic divisibility makes integral theta images short after
normalization. A second moment shows that enough translates of
these images are independent. Finally, selected twists at the
coefficient primes give a lower bound on their corrected determinant.
Write \(D\) for the target dimension, \(n\) for the retained rank,
\(m\asymp\sqrt{x}\) for the number of lifts, \(H\) for the shared main
term, and \(R\) for the source's signed prime remainder.
The product formula, shortness, and the twist correction yield
\[
 nmR\le -(D-n)H+nE_A+E_B+I+Q_0.
\]
The errors record norm, ratio, finite clearing and complement costs.
This formula is proved abstractly, but the arithmetic maps and
estimates realizing it remain to be proved. At the source scales it
would yield a one-sided prime remainder of order
\(x^{-1/2+\varepsilon}\). A Mellin transform and a suitable quadratic
twist then exclude zeros to the right of \(1/2\); the functional equation
excludes the other side.

\section{How to read the mathematics}
The \prooflink{paper/rh/main.tex}{RH manuscript} treats the fixed
conductor-one target using modulus \(18480\); the
\prooflink{paper/grh/main.tex}{Dirichlet manuscript} allows every
fixed target conductor. They share the mathematical body
\prooflink{paper/determinant/body.tex}{here}, rather than repeating
the argument.
The \prooflink{notes/lsernau-general-small-twist.md}{abstract small-twist
theorem} uses classical duality, finite presentations and simultaneous
torsion evaluation; the \prooflink{notes/lsernau-zero-detection.md}
{analytic proof} is complete relative to (1) in the main manuscript.
The \prooflink{blueprint/merged-proof-plan.md}{dependency plan} identifies
the precise open common theta, Selmer, critical-value and determinant
suppliers. The earlier geometric route is archived, not a prerequisite.
The \prooflink{hecke/paper/main.md}{Hecke extension} retains its separate
analytic target and cubic benchmark. Dirichlet GRH alone does not
settle arbitrary Hecke characters.
