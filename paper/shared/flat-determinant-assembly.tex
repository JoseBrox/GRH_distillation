\paragraph{Assembling the prescribed local norms.}
\begin{corollary}\label{geo-flat-norm-assembly}
Suppose the data of Theorem~\ref{geo-flat-family-generator} extend
to a normal integral proper flat
model $\mathcal Y/\mathcal O_K$ with nonempty connected special
fibres. Retain the original global rigidified biextension,
coefficient action and adjunction isomorphisms, with the prescribed
constant signature. Assume that each geometric fibre has the
semiabelian and self-dual abelian-quotient data of
Theorem~\ref{geo-biextension-conjugate-filtration}.
Then a generic generator has point-independent finite norms,
zero logarithmic norm at all but finitely many places.

Retain the other arithmetic and metric hypotheses of
Proposition~\ref{geo-cm-flat-degree}. Suppose the abelian locus has
the point-separating labelled moduli data of
Proposition~\ref{geo-cm-period-point-descent}, and that each pure period
used in Proposition~\ref{geo-cm-integral-product} has the prescribed
pointwise period presentation on the selected complex component.
Then the local-norm comparison required in
Proposition~\ref{geo-cm-flat-degree} follows. Its signed determinant degree is therefore
$(p-q)P_E/2$.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
The global first-order construction and its fibre comparisons give the
actual Hodge bundle and coefficient freeness.
Proposition~\ref{geo-ramified-hodge-determinant} constructs
$\mathcal M_\tau$ with exactly the prescribed tangent eigenlattices
at all integral points, including ramified coefficient primes.
On a special-fibre curve its degree is that of the character determinant.
The constructed Hodge and conjugate filtrations and
Proposition~\ref{geo-frobenius-determinant-torsion} make this degree zero.
Apply Theorem~\ref{geo-vertical-line-rigidity}
at a finite place $v$: for some integers $M_v>0,a_v$,
$s^{\otimes M_v}/\pi_v^{a_v}$ generates the tensor power
on the whole localized model. Every integral point consequently
has logarithmic norm
$(a_v/M_v)\log|\pi_v|_v$, also after a ramified extension.
The divisor of $s$ has finitely many components, so this is zero
at almost all places. Theorem~\ref{geo-flat-family-generator}
gives constant complex norms.
Proposition~\ref{geo-cm-period-point-descent} proves that the exact CM
period points are defined over number fields, preserving the original line,
coefficient action and both finite level maps. After the finite
extensions used to obtain the good product-isogeny models,
their integral points and exact tangent lattices are supplied by
Proposition~\ref{geo-cm-integral-map-extension}. Evaluating the same
constant norms at these points proves the required comparison, and
the CM degree formula applies.
\end{proof}

The remaining construction concerns the actual arithmetic model with
its original boundary-fibre data, the point-separating moduli family
and the required pointwise period presentations. The exact algebraic
CM points, their integral extensions and the tangent-lattice comparison
then follow from the preceding propositions.
