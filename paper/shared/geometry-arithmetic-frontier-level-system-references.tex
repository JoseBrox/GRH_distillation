\bibitem{level-system-projective}
Kai-Wen Lan, \emph{Integral models of toroidal compactifications with
projective cone decompositions}, International Mathematics Research
Notices 2017 (2017), 3237--3280. Proposition 2.8 and Theorem 6.1.
\url{https://www.kwlan.org/articles/cpt-ram-nbl.pdf}.
The cited Proposition 2.8 supplies a smooth projective fan invariant
under the integral compact normalizer.  The proof uses its rational
cones at all principal Siegel levels, whose cusp lattices are homothetic.

\bibitem{level-system-ramified}
Kai-Wen Lan, \emph{Compactifications of PEL-type Shimura varieties
in ramified characteristics}, Forum of Mathematics, Sigma 4 (2016),
e1.  Proposition 4.12, Lemma 4.16, construction (7.7)--(7.10), and
Theorem 7.14.
\url{https://www.kwlan.org/articles/cpt-ram-flat.pdf}.
The construction is finite normalization in the actual generic moduli
fields.  Theorem 7.14 gives independence of auxiliary presentations
with the same induced cone data.  Proposition 13.19 alone does not
assert finite toroidal transitions and is not used for that purpose.
