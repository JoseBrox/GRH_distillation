\bibitem{member-grauert}
H. Grauert, \emph{Ein Theorem der analytischen Garbentheorie
und die Modulr\"aume komplexer Strukturen},
Publications Math\'ematiques de l'IH\'ES \textbf{5} (1960), 5--64,
\S7, Satz~5, p.\ 64, with the flatness and reduced-base
hypotheses on p.\ 62; and \emph{Berichtigung},
ibid.\ \textbf{16} (1963), 35--36.
For a proper holomorphic family over a reduced base and a
coherent sheaf flat over the base, constant fibre cohomology
dimension gives a locally free direct image and fibre base
change.  Here the family is smooth and proper, the sheaf is an
invertible fibrewise trivial line, and $h^0=1$.  The corrigendum
changes intermediate proof details and \S7, Satz~2; it does
not change the invoked Satz~5.
\url{https://www.numdam.org/item/PMIHES_1960__5__5_0.pdf};
\url{https://www.numdam.org/item/PMIHES_1963__16__35_0.pdf}.
