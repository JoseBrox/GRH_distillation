\section{The mixed level identity and the remaining local theta branch}
\label{sec:geometry-mixed-level}

The two translations in the prescribed theta section can be moved
through the original normalized pullbacks without changing a scalar.
Together with reached-section regularity, this supplies the level-prime
branch of the denominator argument.  The final all-place statement
retains the actual model and cover, where those objects still have to
be constructed.

Write $[a]^\natural$ for the original normalized pullback by $a$, and
$T_x^{(k)}$ for the canonical symmetric theta action at odd level $k$.
Both use the fixed rigidification.  Thus the section specified by the
source's \texttt{IsUUOver} relation is exactly
\begin{equation}\label{eq:mixed-original-section}
 U_N(x_1,x_2;s)=T_{x_2}^{(N^2)}T_{x_1}^{(N^2)}[N]^\natural s.
\end{equation}
The order of these translations is part of the formula.

\begin{proposition}[Exact mixed pullback]\label{prop:exact-mixed-pullback}
Suppose $N$ is positive and odd,
\[
 N=\beta\alpha n',\qquad
 \alpha\bar\alpha=A,\qquad\beta\bar\beta=B,
\]
where $\alpha,\beta\in\mathcal O_E$ and $n',A,B$ are positive
integers.  Let $x_1,x_2\in A[N^2]$ satisfy
\[
 [n'^2\alpha\beta]x_1=0,\qquad[n'^2A\beta]x_2=0.
\]
Then $N^2=n'^2AB$, and the explicitly reached section
\begin{equation}\label{eq:mixed-reached-explicit}
 h=T_{\beta x_2}^{(n'^2A)}[\alpha]^\natural
          T_{\alpha\beta x_1}^{(n'^2)}[n']^\natural s
\end{equation}
satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:mixed-exact-factorization}
                 [\beta]^\natural h=U_N(x_1,x_2;s).
\end{equation}
All sections, level changes and scalars are the original ones.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Taking conjugates gives $N^2=n'^2AB$; hence all levels in
\eqref{eq:mixed-reached-explicit} are odd.  The source's restriction on
the intermediate input level holds because $n'^2=1$ or $n'^2\geq4$.
The torsion assumptions are exactly those needed for the two lower-level
translations, so $h$ is reached.

Normalized pullback composes exactly:
$[b]^\natural[a]^\natural=[ab]^\natural$.  The two composite line
isomorphisms have the same rigidification; their ratio is a scalar on
the proper connected abelian variety and is one at zero.  It also
commutes with the prescribed symmetric action:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:mixed-pull-move}
 [a]^\natural T_{ax}^{(k)}=T_x^{(k,a\bar a)}[a]^\natural
                  \quad(x\in A[k,a\bar a]).
\end{equation}
Indeed conjugating the source lift by the normalized line isomorphism
produces a lift at $x$.  In its full odd-level power the line
isomorphism factors telescope, and the source lift has trivial power;
the same conjugation preserves the inversion symmetry.  Uniqueness
of the odd symmetric lift identifies it with the canonical target
lift.  A possible comparison scalar would have both square one and
odd-level power one, hence be one.  This proves
\eqref{eq:mixed-pull-move} with the original normalization.
Apply it first to $\beta$ and $x_2$, then to $\alpha\beta$ and $x_1$,
and compose the pullbacks.  The resulting expression is exactly
\eqref{eq:mixed-original-section}.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[The normalized mixed harmonic term]
\label{cor:mixed-harmonic-term}
Use the original supplied \texttt{Good3} model and chart with
$\nu(H(P;f))\geq-\rho$ for integral harmonic input.  Suppose $n'$ is
a unit in the valuation ring.  Let $P$ have its original row weights
$w(\tau,i)$, be integral in that chart, and have total degree $d$.
For an $n$-tuple of sections as in
\eqref{eq:mixed-original-section},
\[
 \nu\!\left(\operatorname{nrm}(\alpha)N^{-d}
               H(P;U_N(x_1,x_2;s))\right)\geq-\rho.
\]
Here the same factorization is used in every column.
\end{corollary}
\begin{proof}
If $P=0$ the assertion is immediate.  Otherwise
$d=\sum_{\tau,i}w(\tau,i)$.
Theorem~\ref{thm:reached-sections-regular} makes the tuple $h$ integral
with scalar one.  The original harmonic chain rule and
Proposition~\ref{prop:exact-mixed-pullback} give
$H(P;U_N)=\operatorname{nrm}(\beta)H(P;h)$.
The original multiplicative type norm satisfies
\[
 \operatorname{nrm}(\alpha)N^{-d}\operatorname{nrm}(\beta)
          =(n')^{-d},
\]
because $\operatorname{nrm}(N)=N^d$ and
$N=\beta\alpha n'$.  The right side is a local unit.
The chart estimate applied to $h$ proves the assertion.
\end{proof}

\subsection*{A rational factorization at every level prime}

Retain the original self-dual lattice $\Lambda\subset E^n$, its
level-compatible odd $N$ divisible by the original level $M$, and
\[
 I=[\Lambda\cap\mathcal O_E^n:N\mathcal O_E^n]^{p+q}.
\]
Let $\mathcal Z$ be the entire prescribed lattice-annihilator torsion
group, with its original rational enumeration, and suppose
$\Lambda$ is standard locally at the residue prime $\ell$.
Let each $t_b$ satisfy $[N]t_b=\eta_b$, where the original level point
$\eta_b$ is killed by $M$.  Put
\[
 S=\sum_{z\in\mathcal Z}H\bigl(P;(U_N(z_b,t_b;s))_b\bigr).
\]
The harmonic polynomial is the one prescribed by $\Lambda$; local
standardness makes it integral in the original chart.

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:local-level-theta-denominator}
For the same model and chart,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:local-level-theta-denominator}
                  \nu(M^d I^{-1}N^{-d}S)\geq-\rho.
\end{equation}
There is no restriction on $v_\ell(M)$.
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
Write $N=\ell^e n'$ and $M=\ell^k m$, with $k\leq e$,
$(\ell,n'm)=1$, and $m\mid n'$.  Choose the actual rational elements
\[
 \alpha=\ell^k,\quad\beta=\ell^{e-k},\quad
 A=\ell^{2k},\quad B=\ell^{2(e-k)}.
\]
These satisfy the factorization in
Proposition~\ref{prop:exact-mixed-pullback}.  No ideal generator or
new field is needed.  Since $[NM]t_b=0$ and
$NM\mid\beta n'^2A$, its second torsion condition holds.  Also
$NM\mid N^2$, so all $t_b$ are $N^2$-torsion.

Decompose the full finite abelian group exactly as
$\mathcal Z=J\oplus J'$, where $J$ is $\ell$-primary and $J'$ has
order prime to $\ell$.  Local standardness identifies
$J=A[\ell^{v_\ell N}]^n$.  This follows directly from the original
annihilator row relations: their coefficients are integral locally,
and the identity rows occur after multiplication by a local unit.
It gives both $[N]J=0$ and
\[
             |J|=\ell^{v_\ell(I)}=:I_\ell.
\]
The rank here is the Tate-module rank $p+q$ over $E$, not the tangent
rank.  Equivalently one computes both sides by the same local Smith
forms.

The normalized section $[N]^\natural s$ is fixed by canonical
translation by $A[N]$.  Primary and prime-to-$\ell$ theta translations
commute with scalar exactly one: their Weil pairing has order dividing
two coprime integers, and the canonical odd composition scalar then
has square one and odd order.  Therefore the full sections, before
harmonic evaluation, satisfy
\[
 U_N(u+z',t;s)=U_N(z',t;s)\quad(u\in J, z'\in J').
\]
Hence $S=I_\ell\sum_{z'\in J'}H(P;U_N(z',t;s))$.
Each $z'$ is killed by $n'^2$, since it is prime to $\ell$ and killed
by $N^2=\ell^{2e}n'^2$.
Corollary~\ref{cor:mixed-harmonic-term} now bounds each term after
multiplication by $\alpha^dN^{-d}$.  Summation preserves the bound.
The factor $I_\ell/I$ is a local unit.  Finally
$M^d=\alpha^d m^d$ with $m$ a local unit, which proves
\eqref{eq:local-level-theta-denominator}.
The source's intervening type-ideal generator $\beta_H$ is also a unit
at this place: all determinant ideals of $\Lambda$ are local units.
It can therefore be retained on either side of the same estimate.
\end{proof}

\subsection*{Exact all-place assembly, with the model still explicit}

Here is the resulting arithmetic supplier for the original theta
family.  Retain the original good cover, original integral Fourier
weights $a_q$, the exact comparison of its normalized theta section
$\theta=\beta_H g$ with the cover, and all its model/chart hypotheses.
Let $\mathcal Q$ be its finite set of level tuples, $C=|\mathcal Q|$,
and $\Delta=|\operatorname{disc}(E)|$.  At every arithmetic test point,
a permitted finite extension supplies the original model, rational
$N^2$-torsion and the exact identity
\begin{equation}\label{eq:mixed-original-cover-value}
 \theta(y)=\frac{\zeta}{C}
       \sum_{q\in\mathcal Q}a_q I^{-1}N^{-d} S_q.
\end{equation}
Here $S_q$ is the preceding full sum with the original translate
$t_{q,b}$, $[N]t_{q,b}=\eta_{q,b}$, and $t_{q,b}$ is killed by a
power of the half-level ideal.  The phase $\zeta$ is the original
root of unity; it is one for the identity cover used by the theta
supplier.  All $a_q$ are algebraic integers.  Formula
\eqref{eq:mixed-original-cover-value}, including $\beta_H$ in
$\theta=\beta_Hg$, is the source's \texttt{shortExpr}; no factor is
absorbed into a new theta normalization.

Suppose the original harmonic degree bound is $D\geq d$ and the
already proved discriminant chart estimate is
$\rho\leq D\nu(\Delta)$.  For every scalar $s$ satisfying the
original determinant-ideal inclusion
\begin{equation}\label{eq:mixed-clearing-ideal}
 (s)\prod_\tau I_\tau(\Lambda\cap\mathcal O_E^n)^{m_\tau}
       \subseteq\prod_\tau I_\tau(\Lambda)^{m_\tau},
\end{equation}
one has
\begin{equation}\label{eq:mixed-single-clearing-integer}
       Zs\theta\ \hbox{in the original integral cusp lattice},
       \qquad Z=C\Delta^D M^D.
\end{equation}
The scalar $s$ need not be globally integral.

To prove this, consider a finite place over $\ell$ in the original
valuation set.  When $\ell\mid M$, the prescribed lattice is locally
standard.  Condition~\eqref{eq:mixed-clearing-ideal} then gives
$\nu(s)\geq0$.  Theorem~\ref{thm:local-level-theta-denominator}
and $D\geq d$ bound each $sM^D I^{-1}N^{-d}S_q$ below by $-\rho$.
When $\ell\nmid M$, the prescribed $t_{q,b}$ has order prime to
$\ell$, since it is killed by a power of the half-level ideal, which
contains $M$.  Decompose $\mathcal Z$ into primary and prime-to-$\ell$
parts.  For each fixed prime-to-$\ell$ part, take its two prescribed
translates of $[N]^\natural s_0$ as the base tuple for the primary
trace.  They are reached and are fixed by primary $N$-torsion:
the relevant Weil pairings again have coprime orders.  Product
isotropy of $\mathcal Z$ is unchanged.  The adapted lattice theorem
and \eqref{eq:mixed-clearing-ideal} supply
$\nu(s\delta_p)\geq0$.  The full primary estimate, followed by the
sum over the prime-to-$\ell$ part, gives
$\nu(sI^{-1}N^{-d}S_q)\geq-\rho$.
At this place $M$ is a unit.

In both cases $\Delta^D$ clears the chart loss.  Multiply
\eqref{eq:mixed-original-cover-value} by $Zs$; the entire average
denominator $C$ cancels, and the integral weights and root-of-unity
phase preserve nonnegativity.  A finite extension of ramification
index $r$ changes $v$ to $rv$ and the original denominator $e$ to
$re$, so the normalized value $v/e$ is unchanged.  Thus the conclusion
holds in every original valuation test, and membership in the same
integral cusp lattice follows.  It is independent of $N$, $\Lambda$,
$P$, $s$, and the particular theta generator once the original weight
and cover are fixed.  If the source's stated logarithmic bound for
$Z$ is supplied, it applies without the extra doubling used for
$Z^2$.

The exact mixed compatibility and the full local level-prime estimate
are proved above.  The all-place conclusion is an improved sufficient
condition with the original cover and model suppliers still explicit;
it is not an assertion that those geometric objects have already been
constructed.  No whole proof-map node is closed by this section.
The source interfaces and finite normalization checks are recorded
separately\cite{mixed-level-source}.
