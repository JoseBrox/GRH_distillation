\subsection{The established weighted-seed input}
\label{global-gram-established-seed}

For the global summation below we use the following already proved
input, with its original quotient measure and exact reproducer
\cite{global-gram-established-seed-reference}.
Retain an original arithmetic setting, its groups
$G_v=U(k+r,r+1)$ and $S_u=N_u\rtimes H_u$, the prescribed
continuous vector-valued seed $W_u$, and all its Haar measures.
Write $n=2r+k+1$, $q_v=r+1$, and
\[
 M(g)=\prod_{v\mid\infty}\|g_v\|_{\mathrm{op}},\qquad
 \nu_u(g)=\inf_{s\in S_u(F_\infty)}M(sg),\qquad
 N_X=4n^2\sqrt{2\max_vq_v}.
\]

\begin{theorem}[Established weighted-seed theorem]
\label{actual-seed-decay-theorem}
If the original gap conditions
$n\ge1801$ and $b_{j+1}-b_j\ge16n^{19}$ hold at every
infinite place and every prescribed adjacent index, then
\[
 \int_{S_u(F_\infty)\backslash G(F_\infty)}
       \nu_u(g)^{N_X}\|W_u(g)\|\,dq_u(g)<\infty.
\]
There is also a smooth compactly supported scalar function $\alpha$
such that
\[
 W_u(g)=\int_{G(F_\infty)}\alpha(t)W_u(gt)\,dt
 \qquad(g\in G(F_\infty)),
\]
with absolute convergence at every $g$. The original coefficient
kernel also satisfies
\[
                  \int |p(t)|M(t)^{N_X}\,dt<\infty.
\]
All constants may depend on the fixed arithmetic setting.
\end{theorem}

The proof uses the gap for the actual lowest compact type,
weighted Cartan integration, Bessel multiplicity one and its exact
compact reproducer. Its formal degree and Haar factors are retained.
This fixed-setting theorem supplies convergence, without asserting
a uniform small error for the growing arithmetic family.
