\subsection{Weighted integrability of the prescribed global seeds}
\label{actual-seed-decay}

This result supplies the convergence input for the original rational
return expansion.  Its constants are allowed to depend on the fixed
arithmetic data; it asserts no uniform bound for that expansion.
In particular it does not construct the underlying arithmetic setting.

Retain an original setting $X$ and all its Haar measures.  Write
$G_\infty=\prod_{v\mid\infty}G_v$, where
$G_v=U(k+r,r+1)$ and $n=2r+k+1$ is the original moving dimension.
The original gap hypothesis is
\[
 n\ge1801,\qquad b_{j+1}-b_j\ge16n^{19}
\]
at every real place and every prescribed adjacent index.  For an
original arithmetic word $u$, put
$S_u=N_u\rtimes H_u\subset G_\infty$ and
$\lambda_u=\psi_u\pi_u$.  The compact group $H_u$ is the product
of the prescribed $U(k)$ groups, and $\psi_u$ is the original
generic flag character.  Let
\[
 M(g)=\prod_v\|g_v\|_{\rm op},\qquad
 \nu_u(g)=\inf_{s\in S_u}M(sg),\qquad
 N_X=4n^2\sqrt{2\max_v q_v}.
\]
These are exactly the original quotient norm and exponent, with
$q_v$ the negative index at $v$.  Inner products are conjugate
linear in their first argument.  Set
\[
 m(g)=\prod_v\langle w_v,\sigma_v(g_v)w_v\rangle,
 \qquad p(g)=\Bigl(\prod_vd_v\Bigr)\overline{m(g)},
\]
where $w_v$ is the original unit vector in the lowest compact
type and $d_v$ is its formal degree for the original $dg_v$.
The source specifies precisely this $p$, the continuous section
$W_u$, and the identities
\begin{equation}\label{actual-seed-original}
 \begin{gathered}
 W_u(sg)=\lambda_u(s)W_u(g),\qquad
 \int_{S_u\backslash G_\infty}\|W_u\|^2\,dq_u=1,\\
 \int_{G_\infty}p(t)W_u(gt)\,dt=W_u(g).
 \end{gathered}
\end{equation}
The last integral is already required to be absolutely convergent
pointwise in the setting.  The quotient measure $dq_u$ is the
pushforward of its specified representative measure $\nu_Q(u)$;
it uses $ds_u=(\mu_N(u)\times\mu_H(u))_*$ and the given Weil
formula.\cite{seed-decay-original}

\begin{theorem}\label{actual-seed-decay-theorem}
For every original setting satisfying the gap hypothesis,
\begin{equation}\label{actual-seed-weighted}
 \int_{S_u\backslash G_\infty}\nu_u(g)^{N_X}
                       \|W_u(g)\|\,dq_u(g)<\infty
 \qquad\text{for every original word }u.
\end{equation}
Moreover, there are a compact $\Omega\subset G_\infty$ containing
$1$, a smooth compactly supported scalar function $\alpha$, and
a finite $A\ge0$, such that
\[
 \operatorname{supp}\alpha\subset\Omega,\qquad
 |\alpha|\le A,\qquad
 \int_{G_\infty}\alpha(t)W_u(gt)\,dt=W_u(g)
 \quad(g\in G_\infty).
\]
The integrals here are absolutely convergent for every $g$.
Also $p\in L^1(G_\infty)$, with the stronger bound
\begin{equation}\label{actual-seed-coefficient-weight}
             \int_{G_\infty}|p(t)|M(t)^{N_X}\,dt<\infty.
\end{equation}
Consequently these are the full original weighted-seed and compact
reproduction conditions, and the original coefficient kernel is
integrable.  No extra local or global period nonvanishing hypothesis
is needed for this implication from an existing setting.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
\noindent\emph{The actual coefficient has enough decay.}
The ordered parameter at each place is
\[
 (v_1,\ldots,v_{k-1},-b_0,-b_1,\ldots,-b_{2r+1}),
\]
with positive signs on the $v_i$ and alternating positive and
negative signs on the $-b_j$, beginning with positive.  Strict
decrease of the full parameter puts every $v_i$ above $-b_0$.
Thus every pair of opposite signs has difference at least
$h=16n^{19}$: for two tail entries sum adjacent increments, and
for a head entry and a negative tail entry use the increment from
$b_0$ to $b_1$.  This verifies the actual noncompact gap, rather
than a gap for a different parameter.\cite{seed-decay-gap-source}

Put $R=r+1$ and $a=h/(8\sqrt{2R})$.  Here $2R\le n$.
For the quantities in the established smooth-decay theorem,
\[
 A_G\le4n^2,\qquad A_S\le2n^3,\qquad M_{\rm exp}\le6n^2.
\]
Its two remaining numerical lower bounds are at most $79n^5$,
whereas $a\ge2n^{18}$.  Also $h\ge120n^3$.  They hold for
every $n\ge1801$.  Theorem~\ref{original-smooth-decay-from-tensor}
therefore gives, for the actual fixed lowest-type vectors,
\[
 |\langle w_v,\sigma_v(g)w_v\rangle|
                    \le C_v\|g\|_{\rm F}^{1-a}.
\]
The constants and the seminorms producing them need not be uniform.

For completeness, the integrability test used here is elementary.
In a Cartan decomposition of $U(p,q)$, let
$t_1\ge\cdots\ge t_q\ge0$; the present groups have $p\ge q$.
Then $\|g\|_{\rm op}=e^{t_1}$, while the radial Haar density is
bounded by a constant times
\[
 \exp\!\left(2\sum_{i=1}^q(n-2i+1)t_i\right)
                                      \le e^{2pq t_1}.
\]
The remaining $q-1$ coordinates have volume at most a polynomial
of degree $q-1$ in $t_1$.  Hence
$\int_G\|g\|_{\rm F}^{-B}dg<\infty$ for $B>2pq$.
This argument retains the original Haar scalar.  Since
$N_X\le4n^3$ and $a-1-N_X>4n^2$, it proves the weighted
integrability of each factor.  The product formula and Tonelli
give \eqref{actual-seed-coefficient-weight}.  In particular $p$
is integrable because $M\ge1$.

\medskip\noindent\emph{A rank-one projection in the actual induced space.}
Let $\mathcal I_u=L^2(S_u\backslash G_\infty,\lambda_u)$, with
right translation $R(t)F(g)=F(gt)$.  The group $S_u$ is
unimodular: $N_u$ is unipotent and the compact Levi has trivial
positive modular character.  Thus the original Weil quotient
measure is right invariant.  The representative measure in the
source gives this same quotient measure, by the local-section
argument in Section~\ref{archimedean-quotient-mass}.  No measure
is rescaled in passing to $\mathcal I_u$.

Write $\sigma=\bigotimes_v\sigma_v$ and $w=\bigotimes_vw_v$.
Schur orthogonality, with exactly the formal degrees above, gives
\[
 p*p=p=p^*,\qquad
 p*\delta_t*p=\langle w,\sigma(t)w\rangle p.
\]
The second equality follows by integrating the two matrix
coefficients against one another; $\delta_t$ denotes point mass
at $t$.  Consequently $P=R(p)$ is an orthogonal projection and
\begin{equation}\label{actual-seed-corner}
        PR(t)P=\langle w,\sigma(t)w\rangle P.
\end{equation}
These identities concern bounded operators, since $p\in L^1$.

Here is why $P$ has rank at most one.  For $F\in\operatorname{im}P$,
\eqref{actual-seed-corner} shows that
$\sigma(t)w\mapsto R(t)F$ preserves all finite Gram matrices,
up to the scalar $\|F\|^2$.  It therefore extends to a bounded
intertwiner $T_F:H_\sigma\to\mathcal I_u$, and $T_Fw=F$.
Evaluation at the identity on smooth vectors embeds this
intertwining space into
\[
       \operatorname{Hom}_{S_u}(H_\sigma^\infty,V_{\pi_u}).
\]
To justify evaluation, right Lie derivatives on the induced bundle
span its tangent directions.  Local Sobolev estimates make every
smooth induced vector a smooth section and bound evaluation by
finitely many derived Hilbert seminorms.  Evaluation is injective
on intertwiners, since its translates are evaluation at every
group point.  The original generic flag character and the actual
compact type are precisely those of the Bessel multiplicity-one
theorem.  Its bound is one at each real place.  For a finite
product the same bound follows by successively fixing the other
tensor factors; the usual smooth external tensor product is
the completed tensor product of the admissible smooth spaces.
Thus $\dim\operatorname{im}P\le1$.\cite{pieri-family-jsz,pieri-family-cw}

By \eqref{actual-seed-original}, $PW_u=W_u$ in this Hilbert
space and $\|W_u\|_2=1$.  Thus
\begin{equation}\label{actual-seed-projection}
             PF=W_u\langle W_u,F\rangle_{\mathcal I_u}.
\end{equation}
This uses the prescribed nonzero normalized section already
present in $X$; it proves no existence statement for such a section.

\medskip\noindent\emph{Weighted integrability without an additional period map.}
Choose a compact measurable set $E\subset S_u\backslash G_\infty$
such that
$A_E=\int_E\|W_u\|^2dq_u>0$; compact exhaustion and the unit
norm guarantee its existence.  The section $\beta=1_EW_u$ is
bounded and has compact quotient support.  Formula
\eqref{actual-seed-projection} says $P\beta=A_EW_u$.
On an indefinite unitary group
$\|g^{-1}\|_{\rm op}=\|g\|_{\rm op}$, so
\[
 \nu_u(yt^{-1})\le\nu_u(y)M(t),\qquad M(t)\ge1.
\]
The quotient function $\nu_u$ is locally bounded, using local
sections of $G_\infty\to S_u\backslash G_\infty$.  Tonelli and
right invariance of $dq_u$ now give the exact useful majorant
\begin{align*}
 A_E\int\nu_u(x)^{N_X}\|W_u(x)\|\,dq_u(x)
 &\le\int\!\int\nu_u(x)^{N_X}|p(t)|\|\beta(xt)\|\,dt\,dq_u(x)\\
 &\le\left(\int_E\nu_u(y)^{N_X}\|W_u(y)\|\,dq_u(y)\right)
       \left(\int|p(t)|M(t)^{N_X}\,dt\right)<\infty.
\end{align*}
The first integral on the right is finite by compactness and
continuity, and the second by
\eqref{actual-seed-coefficient-weight}.  Division by $A_E>0$
proves \eqref{actual-seed-weighted}.  In particular, no norm bound
for $A_E^{-1}$ has entered any claimed uniform arithmetic estimate.

\medskip\noindent\emph{An exact compact reproducing function.}
The lowest compact type of a discrete series has multiplicity
one.\cite{seed-decay-lowest-type}
Let $K=\prod_v K_v$.  Average a smooth compact approximate
identity on $G_\infty$ under conjugation by $K$, using probability
Haar only for this auxiliary averaging.  The resulting functions
$\eta_j$ remain smooth and compactly supported,
$\sigma(\eta_j)\to1$ strongly, and $\sigma(\eta_j)$ commutes
with $K$.  It therefore acts by a scalar $c_j$ on the actual
lowest compact type containing $w$, and $c_j\to1$.
Choose $c_j\ne0$ and put $\alpha=\eta_j/c_j$.
Then $\sigma(\alpha)w=w$.

The intertwiner $T_{W_u}$ constructed above gives
$R(\alpha)W_u=T_{W_u}\sigma(\alpha)w=W_u$ in $\mathcal I_u$.
Both sides have continuous equivariant representatives: for
the left side use compact support of $\alpha$ and continuity
of $W_u$.  The invariant quotient Haar measure has full support,
so their equality is pointwise on the quotient, and hence on
the whole group.  The same compactness proves absolute
convergence for every $g$.  Taking $\Omega$ to contain the
compact support and $1$, and $A=\sup|\alpha|$, gives the exact
source compact-reproduction condition.  Auxiliary probability
averaging has not altered $dg_v$, $ds_u$, $dq_u$, $d_v$, or $p$.
\end{proof}

The conclusion applies word by word with every moving arithmetic
parameter retained, for ordinary RH and for Dirichlet GRH.  It
removes the actual seed convergence input.  The remaining global
task is the original rational-return unfolding and row comparison,
followed by its uniform estimates; these are not consequences of
qualitative finiteness alone.
