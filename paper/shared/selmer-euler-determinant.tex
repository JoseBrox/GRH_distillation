\subsection{The determinant behind the upper length estimate}
\label{subsec:selmer-euler-determinant}

The numerical upper estimate admits a short determinant proof that allows
integral torsion. Let $\mathcal O$ be a discrete valuation ring with fraction
field $F$, uniformizer $\pi$, and $v_\pi(\pi)=1$.

\begin{proposition}[Torsion cancellation]
\label{prop:selmer-euler-determinant}
Let $M$ be a finite $\mathcal O$-module and $u$ an endomorphism invertible on
$M_F=M\otimes_{\mathcal O}F$. Its kernel and cokernel have finite length, and
\[
 \ell_{\mathcal O}(\operatorname{coker}u)
 -\ell_{\mathcal O}(\ker u)
 =v_\pi\det_F(u\mid M_F).
\]
The determinant is $1$ if $M_F=0$.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Put $T=M_{\mathrm{tors}}$ and $L=M/T$. The module $L$ is finite free and
$u_L$ is injective. The kernel--cokernel sequence gives
$\ker u_M=\ker u_T$ and
\[
 0\longrightarrow\operatorname{coker}u_T
 \longrightarrow\operatorname{coker}u_M
 \longrightarrow\operatorname{coker}u_L\longrightarrow0.
\]
An endomorphism of the finite-length module $T$ has kernel and cokernel of
equal length. Their contributions cancel. Smith normal form on $L$ gives
$\ell_{\mathcal O}(\operatorname{coker}u_L)=v_\pi\det_F(u\mid M_F)$.
\end{proof}

Suppose $F$ has characteristic zero, and extend $v_\pi$ to an algebraic
closure $\overline F$. Let $B$ be a Noetherian local $\mathcal O$-algebra
with the same residue field, $M$ a finite $B$-module, and
$B/\operatorname{Ann}_B M$ finite over $\mathcal O$. For an
$\mathcal O$-algebra homomorphism $\sigma:B\to\overline F$, write
\[
 m_\sigma=\ell_{B_{\ker\sigma}}(M_{\ker\sigma}).
\]
Only finitely many of these multiplicities are nonzero. If
$\sigma(a)\ne0$ whenever $m_\sigma>0$, then
\begin{equation}
 \chi_B(a;M):=\ell_B(M/aM)-\ell_B(M[a])
 =\sum_\sigma m_\sigma v_\pi(\sigma(a)).
 \label{eq:selmer-euler-points}
\end{equation}
Indeed, $M$ is finite over $\mathcal O$, and finite lengths over $B$ and
$\mathcal O$ coincide. The generic finite algebra decomposes into local
Artinian factors. After extending scalars to $\overline F$, each factor
has a composition series with $m_\sigma$ copies of the scalar character
$\sigma$. Separability of the characteristic-zero residue extensions
ensures that this is the same multiplicity as the localized length.
Therefore
\[
 \det_F(a\mid M_F)=\prod_\sigma\sigma(a)^{m_\sigma},
\]
and Proposition~\ref{prop:selmer-euler-determinant} proves
\eqref{eq:selmer-euler-points}. Nilpotents and repeated eigenvalues cause
no difficulty. The kernel term cannot be discarded: on
$\mathcal O/\pi^n$ the zero operator has cokernel length $n$ and Euler
characteristic zero.

\paragraph{The finite module to which this applies.}
Let $A=\bigoplus_j A_j$ be the augmented graded algebra of the marked model,
with $B=A_0$. Write its nonzero-weight equations as
\[
 F_1,\ldots,F_r,F_*\in A_{-1},\quad G_1,\ldots,G_s\in A_{-2},
 \qquad E_1,\ldots,E_{r'}\in A_1,\quad E'_1,\ldots,E'_{s'}\in A_2.
\]
For the rational formal chart $\varphi:A\to F[[z_1,\ldots,z_n]]$, form
\[
 N_0=A_{-2},\qquad N_1=A_2,\qquad N_2=BF_*^2+A_{-2}^2A_2,
\]
\[
 \mathcal R=\bigoplus_{\boldsymbol d\in\mathbb N^3}
 N_0^{d_0}N_1^{d_1}N_2^{d_2}
 t_0^{d_0}t_1^{d_1}t_2^{d_2}\subset A[t_0,t_1,t_2].
\]
Let $J_\varphi$ consist of the elements whose coefficients all belong to
$\ker\varphi$. Let $\mathfrak s$ be generated by
$F_i^2t_0,G_jt_0,E_k^2t_1,E'_lt_1,F_*^2t_2$, in the displayed ranges, and set
\[
 M_{\boldsymbol d}
 =\bigl(\mathcal R/(J_\varphi+\mathfrak s)\bigr)_{\boldsymbol d}.
\]
The weight-zero equations killed by the chart already belong to
$J_\varphi$. For this comparison, let $a\in B$ be a trace representative
with $\varphi(a)\ne0$, as prepared without changing any equation-point
value in Theorem~\ref{thm:selmer-abstract-length}. Retain the model
hypothesis that $\xi(a)\ne0$ at every two-sided equation point.
Let $\mathcal T$ be a
finite set of torus-orbit representatives of solutions on which some
negative-weight and some positive-weight functions are nonzero, and let
$\Sigma=\{\xi|_B:\xi\in\mathcal T\}$ be the set of distinct restrictions.

The graded comparison proved below supplies, for all sufficiently large
$\boldsymbol d$ in the componentwise order, the following hypotheses:
$M_{\boldsymbol d}$ is finite over $B$;
$B/\operatorname{Ann}_B M_{\boldsymbol d}$ is finite over $\mathcal O$;
its generic support is contained in $\Sigma$;
$v_\pi(\xi(a))\ge0$ for $\xi\in\mathcal T$; and
\begin{equation}
 m_\sigma(\boldsymbol d)\le2^{r+1+r'},
 \qquad
 2^{r+r'}\ell_{\mathcal O}(T^-)
 \le\chi_B(a;M_{\boldsymbol d}).
 \label{eq:selmer-euler-retained-inputs}
\end{equation}
Here $T^-$ is the integral negative-weight conormal quotient. The second
inequality is the mixed-intersection lower comparison, proved by the
leading quotient and graded exact sequences below. The support and local
multiplicity assertions follow from the finite-slice argument under the
stated geometric hypotheses of the marked model.

Under these hypotheses, \eqref{eq:selmer-euler-points} gives
\begin{equation}
 \ell_{\mathcal O}(T^-)
 \le 2^{-r-r'}\sum_{\sigma\in\Sigma}
 m_\sigma(\boldsymbol d)v_\pi(\sigma(a))
 \le 2\sum_{\xi\in\mathcal T}v_\pi(\xi(a)).
 \label{eq:selmer-euler-refined-bound}
\end{equation}
The intermediate expression keeps the exact point multiplicities.
Nonnegativity permits the final sum to repeat a degree-zero character
when a covering set contains several points from one orbit. Together with
the conormal pairing this bounds the strict Selmer length. If
$e_{\mathcal O}=v_\pi(p)$, dividing every term by $e_{\mathcal O}$ gives
the normalized lengths and valuations. There is no additional residue
degree: embeddings of each characteristic-zero residue extension have
already been counted separately in \eqref{eq:selmer-euler-points}.
