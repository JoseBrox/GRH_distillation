\subsection{The actual ordinary Hecke determinant}
\label{sec:selmer-hecke-determinant}

Retain the original Hida datum, integral type \(\tau_0\), residual
Hecke summand, weight ring \(A=\mathcal O[[X_D]]\), uniformizers and
unnormalized operators.  The type must satisfy its exact local
correspondence property; its existence is a separate input.  Let
\(M_K\) be the ordinary module already constructed, and, when
\(M_K\ne0\), let \(T_K\subseteq\operatorname{End}_A(M_K)\) be the
\(A\)-algebra generated by the original Hecke family.  Let \(B\)
be the universal ordinary determinant ring, with weight map
\(\lambda:A\to B\).

\begin{proposition}[Arithmetic Hecke-to-deformation map]
\label{prop:selmer-hecke-determinant}
For every original compact auxiliary level with \(M_K\ne0\), there
is a unique continuous surjective map
\[
                 f_K:B\longrightarrow T_K,\qquad f_K\lambda(a)=a.
\]
It has the original Frobenius polynomials and ordered ordinary
characters, and preserves every defining deformation relation.
The resulting actions on the actual modules are compatible with
level inclusions and auxiliary translations.  Every nonzero vector
in their smooth union has a nonzero classical specialization with
a continuous semisimple Galois representation, the required
ordinary flags, prescribed type, and the auxiliary local comparison
for every allowed weak correspondence.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
\emph{The selected Jacquet line.}
At a place above \(\ell\), put \(k=L_w\),
\(N_0=N(\mathcal O_k)\), and
\(z=\operatorname{diag}(\varpi^{n-1},\ldots,1)\).
For a smooth irreducible local component \(\sigma\), the map
\(\sigma\to\sigma_N\) to full upper-unipotent coinvariants is
injective on its original ordinary subspace.  Indeed, a vector
in the kernel is a finite sum of \((u-1)v\).  Averaging over the
increasing compact groups \(N_m=z^{-m}N_0z^m\), which exhaust
\(N(k)\), eventually kills it.  On \(N_0\)-invariants,
\[
 U_z^m v=[N_m:N_0]\,\sigma(z^m)e_{N_m}v.
\]
Invertibility of the original unnormalized \(U_z\) proves
injectivity.

At a good weight-zero point the compact torus characters are
pairwise distinct.  Normalized Jacquet restriction and Frobenius
reciprocity embed \(\sigma\) in a principal series
\(\chi_0\times\cdots\times\chi_{n-1}\), with
\(\chi_i|_{\mathcal O_k^\times}=\theta_{x,i}^{-1}\).
Distinct unit restrictions exclude ratios \(|\cdot|^{\pm1}\),
so this principal series is irreducible.  Its Jacquet filtration
has the Weyl-permuted characters.  Their compact restrictions
are distinct; exact projection onto one compact character
therefore gives a one-dimensional summand
\cite{SelmerHeckeDetBZ}.  This proves scalar action of the
ordinary operators on the selected local line, while retaining
all global type multiplicities.

For \(a_j=\operatorname{diag}(\varpi\,1_j,1_{n-j})\), \(q\) the
residue cardinality, and \(U_0=1\), the exact scalars are
\begin{equation}\label{eq:selmer-hecke-determinant-U}
 U_j=q^{j(n-j)/2}\prod_{i<j}\chi_i(\varpi),\qquad
 b_i:=U_{i+1}U_i^{-1}
       =q^{(n-1)/2-i}\chi_i(\varpi).
\end{equation}
The index in the unnormalized sum is
\(\delta_B(a_j)^{-1}=q^{j(n-j)}\), whereas unnormalized
coinvariants contribute \(\delta_B(a_j)^{1/2}\).  This accounts
for every factor in the formula.

\emph{Separation by classical eigencharacters.}
Decompose the finite classical space into automorphic isotypic
summands for the definite unitary group.  The fixed type factors
through a finite group.  Spherical operators act by scalars on
spherical factors, and the preceding calculation handles every
original ordinary operator.  The residual idempotent selects a
sum of these simultaneous eigenspaces; multiplicity spaces
remain untouched.

If \(0\ne t\in T_K\), choose \(v\) with \(tv\ne0\).  Exact
classical control and good weight-zero density give a point where
\((tv)_x\ne0\); one automorphic projection supplies an eigencharacter
\(\phi:T_K\to\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell\) with \(\phi(t)\ne0\).
These eigencharacters form a separating family \(\Sigma\).
The ring \(T_K\), being an \(A\)-submodule of a finite
\(A\)-module, is finite and compact.  Its prescribed residual
factor is local with the original residue field.  Each \(\phi\)
is continuous: its finitely many integral generators have images
in a finite coefficient field, and its maximal ideal maps into
that field's maximal ideal.

\emph{Galois representations at these actual points.}
The original extension \(L/F\) is unramified at all finite places,
so \([F:\mathbb Q]\ge2\); the excluded case would be an everywhere
finitely unramified imaginary quadratic extension of \(\mathbb Q\).
Compact infinity verifies Labesse's condition \((*)\).
Corollary~5.3 therefore gives the base change
\(\Pi=\boxplus_\alpha\Pi_\alpha\), with discrete conjugate-self-dual
blocks and exact split and unramified local comparison
\cite{SelmerHeckeDetLabesse}.

Every block is cuspidal.  In fact, a M\oe glin--Waldspurger block
\(\operatorname{Speh}(\tau,d)\), \(d>1\), has local cuspidal
support containing repeated unramified twists of the support of
\(\tau\).  At a good place above \(\ell\), uniqueness of cuspidal
support first makes all these factors characters and then forces
a repeated unit character, contradicting distinctness
\cite{SelmerHeckeDetMW}.  Several cuspidal blocks may still occur.

For a block of rank \(m_\alpha\), apply the adopted W05 theorem to
\[
 \pi_\alpha=\Pi_\alpha|\det|^{(m_\alpha-n)/2},\qquad
 \operatorname{algNorm}_{m_\alpha}(\pi_\alpha)
              =\Pi_\alpha|\det|^{(1-n)/2}.
\]
The infinity entries become \((m_\alpha-1)/2-s\), for the
appropriate subset of \(0\le s<n\), and hence are regular
algebraic.  The polarizing character
\(\delta_{L/F}^{m_\alpha}|\cdot|^{m_\alpha-n}\) has the required
real sign; its quadratic factor disappears after norm pullback.
The direct sum \(r_\phi=\bigoplus_\alpha r_{\pi_\alpha}\) has
the original rank-\(n\) normalization and all labeled
Hodge--Tate weights \(0,\ldots,n-1\).

Away from \(\ell\), use the full classical local comparison.
At \(\ell\), the good principal-series parameter has zero
monodromy, so the adopted monodromy-dominance theorem gives the
same comparison there.  Distinct inertia makes its summands
one dimensional, removing any Frobenius-semisimplification
ambiguity.  Unramified comparison at the nonsplit places is
retained, so \(r_\phi\) factors through the original \(G_0\).
Semisimplicity and conjugate self-duality give
\[
                  r_\phi^c\simeq r_\phi^\vee\varepsilon^{1-n}.
\]
This is the required nondegenerate pairing, without an additional
symmetry requirement.  If \(r_\phi\) is irreducible, there is one
rank-\(n\) block, giving the source's conditional cuspidality.

For any allowed weak local correspondence, its toral
zero-monodromy guard identifies its parameter with the classical
one by the previously proved local preimage and uniqueness
argument.  Without the guard, the actual Weil--Deligne
representation is the existence witness.  This supplies exactly
the auxiliary WOT comparison for the entire isobaric sum.
The same transport retains the audited period models at \(\ell\).
At a type place the exact property of \(\tau_0\) gives zero
monodromy and the prescribed diagonal Teichm\"uller inertia.

\emph{Continuous ordinary characters and flags.}
The \(U_j\) are invertible on the finite free \(A\)-module \(M_K\):
their commuting product for \(j<n\) is the ordinary operator, and
\(U_n\) is central translation. Cayley--Hamilton puts each inverse
in \(A[U_j]\), since its determinant is an \(A\)-unit. Hence they
are units of the actual Hecke algebra. Local
reciprocity constructs a continuous \(T_K^\times\)-valued
character \(\xi_{v,i}\) with unit restriction \(\theta_i^{-1}\)
and value \(b_i\) on the fixed uniformizer.  The extension over
the valuation factor exists because \(T_K^\times\) is profinite.
Put
\begin{equation}\label{eq:selmer-hecke-determinant-psi}
 \psi_{v,i}=\xi_{v,i}\varepsilon^{-i},\qquad
 \psi_{v,i}\varepsilon^i\theta_i=1\ \text{on units},\qquad
 \prod_{i<j}\psi_{v,i}\varepsilon^i=U_j\ \text{on }\varpi.
\end{equation}
The original torus and residual identities give
\(\psi_{v,i}|_I=\kappa_{v,i}\), the prescribed torsion restriction,
and the full residual character.

At a good point, \(\psi_{v,i}\) has Hodge--Tate weight \(i\) and
Weil--Deligne Frobenius value
\(b_iq^i=\chi_i(\varpi)q^{(n-1)/2}\).
Here \(\mathrm{WD}(\varepsilon)\) has geometric Frobenius
value \(q^{-1}\); this is not a formula for the cyclotomic
character's value on a local uniformizer at \(\ell\).
After a finite extension killing inertia, twist by
\(\psi_{v,0}^{-1}\).  The designated crystalline line has
Newton number zero.  All Hodge labels are nonnegative, so its
induced Hodge number is nonnegative; weak admissibility makes
it zero.  Every labeled filtration jump on that line is zero.
Undoing the twist identifies it with the crystalline module of
\(\psi_{v,0}\).  Full faithfulness, with descent, gives that
Galois subrepresentation.  Apply the same argument to the
quotient and the successive characters.  This gives the actual
ordered flag \cite{SelmerHeckeDetCF}.

\emph{Integral determinant descent.}
The separating maps embed the compact ring \(T=T_K\) as a closed
subring of \(\prod_{\phi\in\Sigma}\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell\).
The tuples of characteristic coefficients of \(r_\phi(g)\)
are continuous in \(g\).  On the dense original split Frobenius
set they come from
\[
 P_w(X)=\sum_{j=0}^n(-1)^j q_w^{j(j-1)/2}T_{w,j}X^{n-j},
 \qquad T_{w,0}=1.
\]
The source uses inverse arithmetic Frobenius.  Closedness gives
continuous \(T\)-valued characteristic coefficients for every
\(g\), without a uniformity assumption on \(\phi\).

These coefficients determine an integral determinant, not just a
trace.  For universal matrices the formal identity
\[
 \det\left(1-\sum_i t_iA_i\right)
       =\prod_{[w]\ \mathrm{primitive\ cyclic}}\det(1-t_wA_w)
\]
follows over \(\mathbb Q\) by logarithms and grouping traces by
primitive cyclic words, then over \(\mathbb Z\) by torsion-freeness.
Each coefficient of degree at most \(n\) uses finitely many words.
For \(A_i=r_\phi(g_i)\) it is an integral polynomial in the
descended coefficients of \(g_w\).  Multiplicativity, homogeneity
and base-change naturality hold coefficientwise after every
\(\phi\), hence over \(T\).  This constructs the unique continuous
degree-\(n\) determinant on \(T[G_0]\); no division by \(n!\) is
used, even when \(\ell\le n\).

\emph{The exact deformation relations.}
Frobenius density identifies the residual determinant.
Conjugate self-duality gives polarization.  The ordered flag
annihilates
\(\prod_{i=0}^{n-1}(r_\phi(g_i)-\psi_{v,i}(g_i))\):
the rightmost factor lowers the last flag step, and the other
factors lower successive steps.  At a type place,
\(I_u\cap\bigcap_i\ker\bar\chi_i\) acts trivially.  Thus all the
required trace identities hold at every \(\phi\), and separation
proves them over \(T\).  Together with continuity and
\eqref{eq:selmer-hecke-determinant-psi}, these are exactly the
original Pair relations.

Universality gives \(f_K\); the inertial universal property gives
its weight compatibility.  Its image contains \(A\), every
spherical generator by the displayed Frobenius polynomial
(\(q_w\) is a unit at \(\ell\)), and every ordinary generator by
\eqref{eq:selmer-hecke-determinant-psi}.  It is therefore
surjective.  Frobenius density and reciprocity give uniqueness.
Restriction at level inclusions and auxiliary translation
preserve these formulas, so the actions are compatible.
Finally project a nonzero classical specialization onto one
automorphic isotypic summand.  Its auxiliary span retains all
multiplicities and supplies the required equivariant
specialization and WOT comparison.
\end{proof}

This closes the actual Hecke-determinant supplier for each original
fixed type datum \cite{SelmerHeckeDetSource}.  The zero module has
its unique action; no residual nonvanishing is asserted.
The integral type-existence supplier, uniform auxiliary level
maps, patched support and two-sided rigidity remain separate.
There is still no uniform integral Selmer torsion bound.
