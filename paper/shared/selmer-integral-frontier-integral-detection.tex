\paragraph{Returning to the original integral coefficients.}
Let $A=k[[t]]$, $K=k((t))$, with $k$ of odd characteristic.
Retain the group, twist and polarization data of
Corollary~\ref{cor:selmer-paired-detection}, defined over $A$.
Write $E_A,O_A$ for the two block spaces. Suppose a power of $t$
times each even matrix unit lies in $\operatorname{span}_A\rho(H)$,
and the integral $\kappa_i$ are independent modulo boundaries
$d_1Y$ with $Y\in O_A$, $Y^T=-Y$.

Let $D_A$ be the commutative $A$-algebra, free as an $A$-module with basis
$1,\varepsilon_1,\ldots,\varepsilon_r,
\varepsilon'_1,\ldots,\varepsilon'_r,\eta$ and products
$\varepsilon_i\varepsilon'_j=\delta_{ij}\eta$;
every other product of two nilpotent basis elements is zero.
The original paired coefficient module and representation are
\[
 W_A=\eta E_A\oplus\bigoplus_i\varepsilon'_iO_A,
 \qquad
 \rho_1(g)=\left(1+\sum_i\varepsilon_i\kappa_i(g)\right)\rho(g).
\]

\begin{proposition}[Integral paired detection]
\label{prop:selmer-integral-paired-detection}
Let $z:G\to W_A$ be an integral paired cocycle, so that
\[
 z(gh)=z(g)+\chi(g)\rho_1(g)z(h)\rho_1(g)^{-1},
 \qquad z(\sigma g)=s z(g)^T.
\]
If $[\eta]\operatorname{tr}(z(h)\rho_1(h))=0$ for every $h\in H$,
there are $N\geq0$ and $Y\in W_A$ with $sY^T=Y$ such that
\[
          t^Nz(g)=\chi(g)\rho_1(g)Y\rho_1(g)^{-1}-Y
                           \qquad(g\in G).
\]
Here the point, odd family and cocycle are fixed before $N$ is chosen.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
The integral span assumption gives the full even span over $K$.
Independence also extends to $K$: multiply the coefficients of a
relation and its skew odd primitive by a common power of $t$,
then apply integral independence. Thus the fraction-field
determinant theorem applies.

The displayed coefficient module is an actual direct sum over $A$.
For $Z=\eta Z_0+\sum_i\varepsilon'_iZ_i$, multiplication gives
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \rho_1(g)Z\rho_1(g)^{-1}
   &=\eta\left(A_gZ_0+\sum_i[\kappa_i(g),A_gZ_i]\right)
                       +\sum_i\varepsilon'_iA_gZ_i,\\
 [\eta]\operatorname{tr}(Z\rho_1(g))
   &=\operatorname{tr}(Z_0\rho(g))
                       +\sum_i\operatorname{tr}(Z_i\kappa_i(g)\rho(g)).
 \end{aligned}
\]
Indeed, writing $B_g=\sum_i\varepsilon_i\kappa_i(g)$, the
cocycle law makes $(1+B_g)\rho(g)$ a representation, with inverse
$\rho(g)^{-1}(1-B_g)$. Expand $(1+B_g)A_gZ(1-B_g)$;
all products of three nilpotent generators vanish.
These formulas also prove that conjugation preserves $W_A$.

Write $z=\eta\varphi+\sum_i\varepsilon'_i\lambda_i$.
The formulas give exactly the paired trace and cocycle identities
of Corollary~\ref{cor:selmer-paired-detection}. Choose its primitives
$Y_0\in E_K$, $Y_i\in O_K$, with $sY_j^T=Y_j$.
There are finitely many entries, so one $N\geq0$ makes every
$P_j=t^NY_j$ integral. Put
\[
                         Y=\eta P_0+\sum_i\varepsilon'_iP_i.
\]
Then $Y\in W_A$ and $sY^T=Y$. The coefficient formulas and the
fraction-field determinant identity give the asserted boundary
equality in $W_K$. The injection $W_A\hookrightarrow W_K$
makes it an equality over $A$. Empty families, empty second blocks
and zero matrices use the same argument, taking $N=0$ if all
primitives vanish.
\end{proof}

For the normalized arithmetic point, $H$ is the original cyclotomic
kernel and $s=-\chi(c)$, where $c^2=1$. Nontrivial cyclotomic
reduction ensures that some $\chi(g)-1$ is a unit. The original
integral span and odd Selmer independence supply precisely the two
integral assumptions above \cite{alpo-integral-detection}.
The exponent may depend on the cocycle. The finite detecting set
and the uniform count of all congruence solutions are separate
subsequent steps; no lifting of a cocycle modulo $t^m$ was assumed.
