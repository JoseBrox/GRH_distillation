\paragraph{The obstruction module at every truncation.}
Assume that the prime $\ell$ is odd. For the original odd
coefficient module $M_m=O_m$, let
$\pi_w:M_m\twoheadrightarrow Q_{w,m}$ be its prescribed local
quotients. Put
$U_w=\ker(H^1(G_w,Q_{w,m})\to H^1(J_w,Q_{w,m}))$,
where $J_w=G_w$ at an ordinary place and $J_w$ is the original
inertia subgroup at an old place. Define $H_{N,m}$ by pairs
\[
 (z,(y_w)_w),\quad z\in Z^2(\Gamma_N,M_m),\quad
 dy_w=\pi_w\operatorname{res}_wz,
\]
modulo pairs $(dx,(\pi_w\operatorname{res}_wx+u_w)_w)$ with
$[u_w]\in U_w$. All cochains are continuous and
$dx(g,h)=g x(h)-x(gh)+x(g)$.

\begin{proposition}[All obstruction quotients]
\label{prop:selmer-obstruction-quotients}
Under the retained original arithmetic cohomology system,
$H_{N,m}$ is finite and killed by $t^m$. Let
$r_\ell=|\{v:v\mid\ell\}|$ be the number of ordinary places. For
\[
C_2=C_0+C_{\rm aux}+r_\ell C_h+2C_d
\]
and every $m$, all sufficiently large $N$ satisfy
\[
              |H_{N,m}/t^eH_{N,m}|\leq q^{ge+C_2}
                    \qquad(0\leq e\leq m).
\]
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Write $\mathcal T=\prod_wH^1(G_w,Q_{w,m})/U_w$ and let
$K_{2,N,m}$ be the cokernel of the map from global to local
second cohomology. Forgetting the local cochains and, in the
other direction, adjoining a zero global cocycle give an exact
sequence
\[
 0\to\operatorname{coker}(H^1(\Gamma_N,M_m)\to\mathcal T)
 \to H_{N,m}\to\ker(H^2(\Gamma_N,M_m)\to
                  \prod_wH^2(G_w,Q_{w,m}))\to0.
\]
Exactness follows directly by subtracting a global primitive;
surjectivity onto the right kernel follows by choosing local
continuous primitives. If $S_{N,m}$ is the odd Selmer group,
taking orders gives
\[
 |H_{N,m}|=|S_{N,m}|\,|K_{2,N,m}|
 \frac{|H^2(\Gamma_N,M_m)|}{|H^1(\Gamma_N,M_m)|}
 \prod_w\frac{|H^1(G_w,Q_{w,m})|}
                 {|U_w|\,|H^2(G_w,Q_{w,m})|}.
\]

Put $R=q^{mab}$. The odd module has order $R^2$, its real
fixed modules have order $R$, and each ordinary quotient has
order $R$. The last assertion uses its $ab$ lower coordinates
between distinct block types in the actual ordinary basis.
Global and local Euler identities, the old unramified count,
and $\sum_vd_v=d$ therefore imply
\[
 |H_{N,m}|\leq |S_{N,m}|
       \prod_{v\mid\ell}|H^0(G_v,Q_{v,m})|\,|K_{2,N,m}|.
\]
The supplied global duality bounds $|K_{2,N,m}|$ by the
cyclotomically equivariant additive dual. Proposition
\ref{prop:selmer-second-bound-suppliers} and auxiliary descent give
\[
 |H_{N,e}|\leq q^{ge+C_0+C_{\rm aux}+r_\ell C_h+C_d},
 \qquad |K_{2,N,e}|\leq q^{C_d},
\]
simultaneously for $e\leq m$ at sufficiently large level.

Use both coefficient sequences
\[
 0\to A_{m-e}\xrightarrow{t^e}A_m\to A_e\to0,\qquad
 0\to A_e\xrightarrow{t^{m-e}}A_m\to A_{m-e}\to0.
\]
They are exact on the global odd modules and on their free
local-coordinate quotients. Their maps on the obstruction modules are
\[
 i:H_{N,m-e}\to H_{N,m},\qquad
 p:H_{N,m}\to H_{N,e},\qquad
 r:H_{N,m}\to H_{N,m-e},
\]
and $ir=t^e$.

We have $\ker p\subseteq\operatorname{im}i$. To see this, lift
a boundary pair representing the image of a class in $\ker p$.
Its allowed local cocycles lift: at ordinary places lift their
boundary primitives; at old places use the original unramified
lifting property, which in the Galois case is lifting one
Frobenius value. Subtract this pair and lift both remaining
components through their exact coefficient kernels.

Also
\[
 |H_{N,m-e}/r(H_{N,m})|\leq |K_{2,N,e}|.
\]
Indeed, the original global right exactness in degree two lifts
the global cocycle component. For the actual odd-primary Galois
coefficients it follows from degree-three vanishing
\cite[I, Theorem 4.10(c)]{milne-adt}. Arbitrary lifts of the local
one-cochains can fail their differential equations only by local
two-cocycles with coefficients in $A_e$. Their classes modulo
global second cohomology give the defect in $K_{2,N,e}$. Equal
defects for two chosen representatives imply that their difference
lifts, giving an injection of the displayed quotient set into
$K_{2,N,e}$. No canonical homomorphism is needed.

Finally, filter $H_{N,m}/t^eH_{N,m}$ by
$\operatorname{im}i/t^eH_{N,m}$. Its quotient has at most
$|H_{N,e}|$ elements, and that submodule has at most
$|K_{2,N,e}|$ elements. Multiplying these bounds proves the
claim, including coefficient length zero
\cite{alpo-obstruction-quotients}.
\end{proof}

Lemma~\ref{lem:selmer-finite-obstruction} now applies with this
one $C_2$. The remaining step identifies the obstruction attached
to the actual even cocycle tuples and translates its vanishing
into the prescribed second-order lift.
