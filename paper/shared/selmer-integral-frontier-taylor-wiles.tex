\subsection{Actual Taylor--Wiles modules and comparison maps}
\label{sec:selmer-taylor-wiles}

Retain the original ordinary Hecke datum, its integral type, weight
algebra \(\Lambda=\mathcal O[[X_1,\ldots,X_D]]\),
\(D=n[F:\mathbb Q]\), and universal ordinary ring \(B\).
Let \(M_0\) be its actual finite free ordinary module, with the
Hecke determinant action, and \(J=\operatorname{Ann}_B M_0\).
This is the original kernel ideal when \(M_0\ne0\), and \(J=B\)
in the source's zero-module branch.

\begin{theorem}[The arithmetic auxiliary-module supplier]
\label{thm:selmer-taylor-wiles}
For every original Taylor--Wiles datum with its prescribed arithmetic
realization, the actual ordinary modules supply all fields of
\(\mathrm{TWAut}\).  The two comparison maps have the exact
multiplier
\[
 f(U)=\prod_j\prod_{i\ne k}
                (U_{ji}-U_{jk})(U_{ji}-Q_jU_{jk}),
\]
and both determinant relation families have exponent \(n!\),
independent of the auxiliary level.  The ideal \(J\), weight
coordinates, ordered residue lifts and original coefficient ring
are retained.  No nonvanishing assertion is included.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Fix the auxiliary level \(N\).  At its prescribed distinct split
places \(w_j\), put \(Q_j=\#k_{w_j}\) and
\(a_j=v_\ell(Q_j-1)\ge N\).
Let \(K_j=\mathrm{GL}_n(\mathcal O_{L_{w_j}})\), let \(I_j\)
be the upper Iwahori, and let \(I_{1j}\) have diagonal residues
in the prime-to-\(\ell\) subgroup.  With products over \(j\),
\[
 \Delta=I/I_1=\prod_{j,i}\mathbb Z/\ell^{a_j}\mathbb Z,\qquad
 \Lambda[\Delta]=
 \Lambda[[Y_{ji}]]/
       \bigl((1+Y_{ji})^{\ell^{a_j}}-1\bigr)_{j,i}.
\]
The actual ordinary smooth union \(\mathcal P\), with the fixed
Hecke set outside the enlarged ramification set, has
\(M_1=\mathcal P^{I_1}\) finite free over this local group ring.
The unnormalized diamond trace onto \(W=\mathcal P^I\) is
surjective with kernel \((Y_{ji})M_1\).

\emph{Coefficients and the base residual block.}
The enlarged ordinary determinant ring \(B_N\) is an actual complete
Noetherian local \(\mathcal O\)-algebra.  Number-field Galois groups
with finite allowed ramification satisfy Mazur's finiteness
condition.  Chenevier's determinant representability theorem
therefore applies, including when \(\ell\le n\)
\cite[Propositions 3.3 and 3.7, Example 3.6]{SelmerTWChenevier}.
Adjoin the finitely many local character deformation rings and
impose the exact prescribed-unit, polarization, ordered ordinary
and type-inertia equations.  These are closed equations in a
Noetherian ring.  The pulled-back original pair supplies a point
over \(B\), so their quotient is proper.  Universality gives
\(\beta:B_N\to B\), preserving both the determinant and ordinary
characters.

The determinant theorem acts on \(\mathcal P\), and its good
classical maps separate vectors.  On \(\mathcal P^K\), those
classical representations are spherical at every \(w_j\); their
Galois representations are unramified there.  Separation descends
the determinant integrally to the original Galois quotient.
The omitted spherical operators satisfy
\[
 \det(X-\rho(\phi_j))
   =\sum_{k=0}^n(-1)^k Q_j^{k(k-1)/2}T_{j,k}X^{n-k}.
\]
Thus they lie in the same finite local Hecke algebra and already
have the prescribed residual values.  Imposing their residual
conditions removes no vectors.  Consequently
\(\mathcal P^K=M_0\), with \(B_N\)-action through \(\beta\).
This verifies the residual-block compatibility that deleting
operators alone would not supply.

\emph{The integral maps.}
Let \(x_j\) and \(\delta_j\) be the exact Artin images of the
prescribed geometric Frobenius \(\phi_j\) and inertia generator
\(\tau_j\).  The integral Iwahori--Matsumoto relations make all
standard pro-\(p\) Iwahori elements invertible since \(Q_j\) is a
unit.  Positive diagonal double cosets multiply without a factor;
quotients of positive diagonals give the commuting operators
\(\Theta\) on each \(I_C\)-invariant module
\cite[Theorem 1, Propositions 4 and 6]{SelmerTWVigneras}.
Their restriction from pro-\(p\) Iwahori invariants retains the
actual unnormalized sums, with no division by \(|C|\).
For \(n=1\) this is ordinary torus translation.  Put
\[
 U_{ji}=Q_j^i\Theta(e_ix_j),\qquad
 1+Y_{ji}=\Theta(e_i\delta_j),\qquad 0\le i<n.
\]
On a normalized Jacquet character \(\xi\), these have eigenvalues
\(Q_j^{(n-1)/2}\xi_i(x_j)\) and \(\xi_i(\delta_j)\):
the modulus exponent \((n-1)/2-i\) cancels the inserted \(i\).
These are exactly the parameters of
\(\pi_j|\det|^{(1-n)/2}\).

Here is an integral construction of both spherical comparison maps.
For one place set
\[
 P=\mathcal O[Z_0,\ldots,Z_{n-1}],\quad S=P^{\mathfrak S_n},
 \quad D_Z=\prod_{i<k}(Z_k-Z_i),\quad
 N_Q=\prod_{i<k}(Z_k-QZ_i),\quad
 d(h)=\frac{\operatorname{Alt}(h)}{D_Z}.
\]
The quotient defining \(d\) is integral.  The Bernstein polynomial
action and the unnormalized spherical trace \(E=\sum_wT_w\) give
\[
 T_i h=Q s_i h+(Q-1)Z_i\frac{h-s_i h}{Z_i-Z_{i+1}},
 \qquad Eh=d(N_Qh).
\]
The Bernstein basis proves these identities in the actual Hecke
algebra; no square root of \(Q\) is needed
\cite[\S\S1.6--1.7, 1.15 and 2.3]{SelmerTWHKP}.
For each subset \(A\subseteq\{(i,k):i<k\}\), put
\[
 a_A=\prod_{(i,k)\in A}Z_k,\qquad
 c_A=\prod_{(i,k)\notin A}(-Z_i).
\]
Direct expansion yields
\[
 \sum_Aa_Aw(c_A)=D_Z\,1_{w=1},\qquad
 \sum_Aa_Ad(c_Ah)=h,\qquad
 \sum_Aa_AEc_A=N_Q.
\]
Indeed a nonidentity permutation has \(i<w(i)\), giving a zero
factor \(Z_{w(i)}-Z_{w(i)}\) in the first expression.
Since \(P\) is finite free over \(S\), these identities exhibit the
perfect pairing \(d(hh')\); its dual tensor intertwines the two
\(P\)-actions on \(P\otimes_SP\).

Let \(\mathcal S\) be the finite ordered-root algebra over \(B\)
for the polynomials \(D_B(X-\phi_j)\), with roots \(z_{ji}\).
Using the product \(\operatorname{Tr}:W\to M_0\) of actual spherical
traces, define
\[
 \Phi(p\otimes m)=p(U)m,\qquad
 \Psi(w)=\sum_{\boldsymbol A}\prod_j a_{A_j}(z_j)\otimes
       \operatorname{Tr}\!\left(\prod_jc_{A_j}(U_j)w\right).
\]
The spherical polynomial identity makes \(\Phi\) well defined.
The dual-tensor identity makes \(\Psi\) intertwine every \(U_{ji}\).
All maps commute with \(B_N\), acting on the left through \(\beta\).
The preceding identities give
\[
 \Phi\Psi=\prod_jN_{Q_j}(U_j),\qquad
 \Psi\Phi=\prod_jN_{Q_j}(z_j).
\]
For the second equality use \(Ec(U)m=d(N_Qc)(U)m\) on a
\(K\)-fixed vector.  The integral polynomial
\(f/\prod_jN_{Q_j}\) multiplies \(\Psi\) to make both compositions
exactly \(f\).  No spherical index or Poincar\'e polynomial is
inverted.

\emph{The one uniform exponent.}
For every irreducible smooth local \(\pi\), its \(I_C\)-invariants
inject into its upper Jacquet module: an element of the Jacquet
kernel is killed by sufficiently deep unipotent averaging, hence
by a power of an invertible positive \(\Theta\)-operator.
A Jacquet character quotient embeds \(\pi\) into a principal
series.  Exact Jacquet restriction and its Weyl filtration imply
\[
                         \dim\pi^{I_C}\le n!
\]
\cite[Proposition 2.3 and Lemma 2.12]{SelmerTWBZ}.
Repeated characters count with multiplicity.

At a separating good classical point, write
\((\alpha_i,\beta_i)\) for a joint character of
\((U_{ji},1+Y_{ji})\).
If \(f_j(\alpha)\ne0\), the \(\alpha_i\) are distinct and no
ratio is \(Q_j\) or \(Q_j^{-1}\).  Local monodromy is therefore
zero.  Exact local compatibility identifies the actual
\(\rho(\phi_j),\rho(\tau_j)\) with simultaneous eigenvalues
\((\alpha_i,\beta_i)\); distinct Frobenius eigenvalues and finite
inertia remove the semisimplification ambiguity.  Consequently
\[
 \prod_k(\rho(\phi_j)-\alpha_k)=0,\qquad
 \prod_{k\ne i}(\rho(\phi_j)-\alpha_k)
                         (\rho(\tau_j)-\beta_i)=0.
\]
If \(f_j(\alpha)=0\), multiplying by that factor already kills
the scalar evaluation.  After left multiplication by \(\rho(g)\)
and taking traces, simultaneous triangularization on a space
of dimension at most \(n!\) kills the \(n!\)-th power.
The other local \(f_k\) commute and add no exponent.
Global multiplicity spaces are retained with identity action.
Classical separation therefore gives, integrally and for every
\(g,j,i\),
\begin{align*}
 \left(f(U)\operatorname{tr}D_N
       \left(g\prod_k(\phi_j-U_{jk})\right)\right)^{n!}M_1&=0,\\
 \left(f(U)\operatorname{tr}D_N
       \left(g\prod_{k\ne i}(\phi_j-U_{jk})
                             (\tau_j-1-Y_{ji})\right)\right)^{n!}M_1&=0.
\end{align*}

\emph{Completion and all coefficient maps.}
Fix the source's ordered lifts \(u^0_{ji}\in\mathcal O\) of
\(\bar\chi_i(T.sg_j)\).  Take the common Fitting summand
\(M_N\subseteq M_1\) on which every \(U_{ji}-u^0_{ji}\) is
topologically nilpotent.  The source's \texttt{T.ph\_H} and
\texttt{TWPl.H\_eq}
make these lifts a residue-root tuple for \(D_B(X-\phi_j)\).
Its commuting projectors are
\(\Lambda[\Delta]\)-linear, so \(M_N\) is finite free over this
local ring.  Set
\[
 R_N=B_N[[Z_{ji},Y_{ji}]],\qquad U^{\rm ring}_{ji}=u^0_{ji}+Z_{ji},
\]
letting \(Z_{ji}\) act by \(U_{ji}-u^0_{ji}\) and \(Y_{ji}\) by
diamond minus one.  The complete finite Hecke algebra makes this
power-series action convergent.  Extend the original determinant
pair by the coefficient inclusion.

The source ring \(\mathrm{Sp}\) is the complete local factor
\(\mathcal S^\wedge_{u^0}\) at the ordered residue roots.
Lifting the residue idempotents selects matching summands of
the two maps, and the exact diamond trace gives
\[
 \mathrm{Sp}\otimes_B M_0
      \ \underset{\Psi}{\stackrel{\Phi}{\rightleftarrows}}\
                         M_N/(Y_{ji})M_N.
\]
Their compositions remain \(f\).  Define the coefficient map by
\[
 \theta:R_N\longrightarrow\mathrm{Sp},\qquad
 b\longmapsto\beta(b),\quad Z_{ji}\longmapsto z_{ji}-u^0_{ji},
 \quad Y_{ji}\longmapsto0.
\]
It preserves the determinant, the ordered ordinary characters and
the root operators.  On \(\Lambda[[Y_{ji}]]\) it is exactly the
constant-coefficient map followed by the original weight map.
The unchanged weight formulas
\(X_k=c_k\psi_{v_k,i_k}(\gamma_k)-1\) verify both weight fields.
The displayed relation powers restrict to \(M_N\).
Finally the arithmetic realization map is continuous, injective
and surjective between compact Hausdorff groups; transport by
this isomorphism retains every original local map, Frobenius
representative and inertia generator.  These constructions give
every field of the original contract \cite{SelmerTWSource}.
Empty auxiliary sets and zero modules are included.
\end{proof}

This closes the actual auxiliary-module and comparison supplier.
Patched support, the arithmetic seed, two-sided rigidity and
the moving-family integral Selmer bound remain separate.
