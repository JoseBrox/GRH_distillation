\section{The original integral types}
\label{sec:selmer-integral-type}

Fix the original Hida setting, rank \(n\geq1\), coefficient DVR \(O\),
residual datum and embedding
\(\iota:\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell\simeq\mathbb C\).
Put \(E_0=\operatorname{Frac}(O)\).
At each original type place \(u\), write
\(F_u=L_{w(u)}\) and \(K_u=\mathrm{GL}_n(O_{F_u})\).
Its residue characteristic is different from \(\ell\).
The characters \(\chi_{ui}\) are the restrictions of the original
Teichm\"uller lifts to the local decomposition group.

\begin{theorem}[Integral types over the original ring]
\label{thm:original-integral-type}
There is a finite free \(O\)-lattice \(V\), of positive rank \(m\),
with an open-kernel action
\(\tau_0:\prod_uK_u\to\mathrm{GL}_m(O)\), satisfying the original
\texttt{TypeForAlt} condition.
Explicitly, if \(M\ne0\) is a complex representation of
\(\prod_u\mathrm{GL}_n(F_u)\) that is isotypic for an irreducible
smooth representation \(\pi_u\) on each factor, then
\[
 \exists(F_i)\ne0:\quad
 \sum_j\iota(\tau_0(k)_{ij})\rho(k)F_j=F_i\quad(k,i)
\]
if and only if, at every \(u\) and for every allowed weak local
correspondence \(R'\),
\[
 \begin{gathered}
 N\!\left(R'(\operatorname{algNorm}\pi_u)\right)=0,\qquad
 R'(\operatorname{algNorm}\pi_u)|_{I_{F_u}}
       \simeq\bigoplus_i\iota\chi_{ui}|_{I_{F_u}},\\
 \operatorname{algNorm}\pi_u=\pi_u|\det|^{(1-n)/2}.
 \end{gathered}
\]
Repeated inertial characters and arbitrary conductor are allowed.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
First work at one local field \(F\). For the classical correspondence put
\[
 r_\ell(\pi)=\iota^{-1}
       \operatorname{rec}_F\!\left(\iota\pi|\det|^{(1-n)/2}\right),
 \qquad \tau=\bigoplus_i\chi_i|_{I_F},\quad N=0.
\]
Shotton's Theorem~3.7(1),(2), proved by his local construction and
Corollary~6.21, gives a smooth irreducible compact type \(\sigma(\tau)\).
Its occurrence implies that the full inertial parameter of
\(r_\ell(\pi)\) is dominated by \(\tau\); equality implies occurrence.
These clauses hold for every irreducible admissible \(\pi\).
The separate genericity clause is unused
\cite{SelmerTypeShotton}.

For every basic inertial constituent of multiplicity \(a\), the partition
of the target is \((1,\ldots,1)\) with \(a\) entries.
A partition dominated by this one has largest part at most one and hence
equals it. Thus
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:original-type-local}
 \operatorname{Hom}_K(\sigma(\tau),\pi)\ne0
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 N(r_\ell(\pi))=0,\qquad r_\ell(\pi)|_{I_F}\simeq\tau.
\end{equation}
The full simple type construction applies also to wild characters and
small residue characteristic. No global patching theorem is used here.
For an orientation check, the trivial rank-two inertial target with
\(N=0\) has partition \((1,1)\) and trivial compact type. The Steinberg
parameter has partition \((2)\), so is excluded.

The compact type is finite dimensional and factors through a finite
quotient \(H\). Its matrices have a model \(\sigma_E\) over a finite
extension \(E/E_0\). For a full \(O_E\)-lattice \(L_0\), the finite sum
\[
                  L=\sum_{h\in H}\sigma_E(h)L_0
\]
is a stable full lattice. The original \(O\)-lattice
\(\mathcal L=\operatorname{Res}_{O_E/O}L\) is free of rank
\([E:E_0]\dim_E\sigma_E\).
After extending scalars,
\[
 \mathcal L\otimes_O\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell
 \simeq\bigoplus_{\substack{\eta:E\hookrightarrow\overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell\\
                         \eta|_{E_0}=1}}\sigma_E\otimes_{E,\eta}
                                  \overline{\mathbb Q}_\ell .
\]
Each \(\eta\) extends to an automorphism fixing \(E_0\).
The normalized correspondence \(r_\ell\) commutes with these
automorphisms, by Henniart \S7.4 in the normalization of Shotton \S3.4
\cite{SelmerTypeHenniart,SelmerTypeShotton}.
Since \(\tau\) is defined over \(E_0\), every conjugate summand satisfies
the same equivalence \eqref{eq:original-type-local}.
Restriction of scalars therefore introduces no extraneous inertial
parameter. The source permits reducible compact types.

Use \(V_u=\mathcal L^\vee\) for the coefficient lattice.
Indeed the source's covariance law for a tuple \(F_i\) is equivalent to
\[
                    \pi(k)F_i
                        =\sum_j\tau_0(k)^{-1}_{ij}F_j,
\]
which says that \(e_i^*\mapsto F_i\) intertwines \(V_u^\vee\) with
\(\pi\). This fixes the dual convention without changing \(O\).

The target inertial characters extend to \(G_F\), so Frobenius preserves
each of their isotypic spaces. Frobenius semisimplicity diagonalizes each
space, even when characters repeat. A parameter with this inertia and
\(N=0\) is therefore toral. The established good-preimage and restricted
uniqueness argument identifies it with the classical parameter for every
allowed weak correspondence. This proves the full local quantifier in
the theorem.

Teichm\"uller lifts exist and are unique in \(O\): Hensel's lemma gives
the unique lift of each element of \(k^\times\) to a root of
\(X^{|k|-1}-1\), and uniqueness makes the lifting multiplicative.
Their kernels equal the original residual kernels. Thus the
universal Teichm\"uller-lift clause introduces no new choice.

Finally take
\[
 V=\bigotimes_{u,\,O}V_u,\qquad
 m=\prod_u [E_u:E_0]\dim_{E_u}\sigma_u.
\]
The product kernel is open. The isotypy assumption gives
\(M\simeq(\bigotimes_u\pi_u)\otimes W\) with \(W\ne0\):
apply evaluation from each irreducible factor and its multiplicity
space in turn, using Schur's lemma and cyclicity. The other factors act
on that multiplicity space; it is a submodule for each of them and
remains isotypic. The compact Hom space for the tensor product is
nonzero exactly when all local Hom spaces are nonzero.
For an empty set of type places, take the rank-one trivial lattice.
This is precisely the original source contract
\cite{SelmerTypeSource}.
\end{proof}

\paragraph{Scope.}
This closes the integral type-existence subobligation used in the actual
ordinary Hecke determinant construction. It retains the original
coefficient ring and every local and residual character.
The displayed rank expansion is recorded, not bounded uniformly in
the moving arithmetic data.
Nonzero residual automorphic spaces, the remaining Taylor--Wiles level
maps, patched support, two-sided rigidity and uniform integral Selmer
bounds remain separate obligations. The whole Selmer node is open.
