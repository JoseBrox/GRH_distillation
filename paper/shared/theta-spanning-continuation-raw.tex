\subsection{Spanning by the actual raw theta sections}
\label{actual-raw-theta-spanning}

The raw spanning problem uses the identity cover. This specialization
removes the auxiliary change of the lower rows: its auxiliary ideal is
$\mathcal O_E$, its level subgroup $J$ is trivial, and its
$\phi^F$ is exactly the finite vector $\Phi$ of
Theorem~\ref{actual-finite-theta-system-theorem}.
The following supplies the original
\texttt{HolSub0LeSpanRaw} contract.\cite{actual-sw-source}

\begin{theorem}\label{actual-raw-theta-spanning-theorem}
Fix the original Host, datum, level, coefficient field $K=c.F$,
connection and normalized complex uniformization $U$ of
the established normalized-Member construction
\cite{current-construction-record}. Suppose $r\ge1$,
$l.A=1$, all component spaces have the same Hermitian matrix and
$\xi_0$, the original principal congruence groups at $M=l.M$ are
contained in their arithmetic groups, and the original weight
character is trivial on every element of each arithmetic group
($\mathrm{ChiOne}$). Let $b>0$, let $(a,n)$ satisfy the original
$\mathrm{Dom}(a,n)$, and suppose
\[
                        n\ge4[E:\mathbb Q]r+4.
\]
Every element of the original $\operatorname{holSub}_0$ is a
finite complex linear combination of readings of the actual raw
sections $\mathcal T_{\Lambda,N,\widehat{1_{x_0}}}(P)$ of
Theorem~\ref{thm:actual-raw-theta-existence}, with
\[
 \Lambda\text{ unimodular and standard at }bM,\qquad
 \mathrm{LevOK}(\Lambda,N,M),\qquad
 P\in\operatorname{harmL}_K(\Lambda,a),
\]
and with $x_0$ spanning the original complementary half-level
quotient. Their values satisfy the literal raw rule over every
$K$-algebra. No field extension or rescaling of that rule occurs.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Write $\mathfrak m=\mathrm{Mfr}(l)$ and $S_L=
\mathfrak m\bar{\mathfrak m}^{-1}L$. In the identity cover
$p_\delta=1$, $I_\delta=\mathcal O_E$, $l_2=l$ and
$\varphi_\delta=1$. The original classical level construction
therefore takes $J$ to be the kernel of the identity map on
$\mathrm{GL}_r(\mathcal O_E/\mathfrak m)$. Thus $J=\{1\}$
and $\lambda=1$. Both $\Phi$ and $\phi^F$ impose zero on
the lower rows modulo $\mathfrak m$, so they agree term by term.
In particular no moving-lattice comparison is required here.

\textbf{The component lattices and their phases.}
Choose $r$ elements of the original source lattice whose classes
form a basis modulo $\mathfrak m$, and take the remaining entries
of $x_0$ to be zero. Such a basis exists because a projective
module of rank $r$ over the finite product of local rings
$\mathcal O_E/\mathfrak m$ is free. The actual dual half-level
marking identifies this quotient with $L/\mathfrak mL$ on each
component. Move each component lattice by a rational unitary
matrix so that these marked elements become the prescribed
$\beta_i$ modulo $\mathfrak m$ and its lattice belongs to
$\mathcal L$.

For clarity, the approximation used in this step requires no
extension of $E$. At a prime of $\mathfrak m$, choose the
matrix taking the two local marked bases to each other. The
conjugate prime is distinct, and the unitary equation determines
the conjugate matrix; self-duality then also matches the
conjugate lattices. These finitely many local unitary matrices
can be approximated by one rational unitary matrix. Indeed the
Cayley map $T\mapsto(1-T)(1+T)^{-1}$ identifies a dense open
set of the unitary group with a dense open set of its
skew-adjoint vector space. Move the local targets, if necessary,
within their prescribed congruence neighborhoods into this open
set, and apply ordinary weak approximation to its coordinates.
The resulting rational matrix satisfies the same congruences.

Denote the resulting lattices by $L_i$. They are in distinct
$\Delta$-orbits: an element of $\Delta$ carrying $L_i$ to
$L_j$ preserves the marked basis modulo $\mathfrak m$.
The perfect alternating pairing carries this to equality of the
original complementary half-level marking modulo $M$, and the
normalized uniformization dictionary identifies the components.
This uses the original half-level marking, not a full-level
marking chosen independently.

The original multiplier $\mu$ is one on the stabilizer of each
$L_i$. Such a stabilizer element is principal at $M$ on $L_i$:
the congruence on one half and its dual on the conjugate half
give the congruence modulo $M$. Its $E$-determinant is one.
Indeed that determinant is an integral unit of modulus one at
every embedding; if it differs from one, the norm of its
difference from one is at least $M^{[E:\mathbb Q]}$ and at
most $2^{[E:\mathbb Q]}$, contrary to $M\ge3$.
The actual $w_0^4$-adapted symplectic marking makes the two
off-diagonal blocks divisible by eight and the relevant
determinant congruent to one modulo eight. The squared theta
transformation is therefore one. Since $n$ is even, the
original $n$-fold vacuum is fixed. These are precisely the
phase cancellations established in the normalized member
construction; $\lambda=1$ contributes no new factor.

\textbf{The exact raw reading.}
Set
\[
 m_\Lambda=[\Lambda:\Lambda\cap\mathcal O_E^n],\qquad
 C=M^{[E:\mathbb Q]rn/2}.
\]
At a component lattice $L$, let $v$ be its standard-lattice
vacuum and $v_{0,L}$ the chosen unit vacuum for
$\mathcal O_E^n\otimes S_L$. Write $P_A$ for the original
orthogonal vacuum projection. We use the original convention
$\langle v,w\rangle=\int v\bar w$, linear in its first
variable; thus $P_{\mathbb Cw}v=\langle v,w\rangle w$
for a unit $w$. Because $\Lambda$ is standard
at the primes of $M$, its modification of the standard lattice
is supported away from the modification $L\mapsto S_L$.
The finite Heisenberg projections consequently give the exact
identity
\begin{equation}\label{raw-span-projection}
 P_{\Lambda\otimes S_L}P_{\Lambda\otimes L}v
   =\langle v,v_{0,L}\rangle\,
       m_\Lambda^{-r/2}v_{\Lambda,L}.
\end{equation}
One can check it without a choice of local tensor factors:
Bezout for their coprime support integers decomposes each
intermediate lattice as the sum of its intersections with the
other two. Averaging the common semicharacter then gives
$P_CP_BP_A=P_CP_A$ for each of the two decompositions.
The remaining projection has positive norm $m_\Lambda^{-r/2}$
by the exact tensor-index identity. The factor
$\langle v,v_{0,L}\rangle$ is nonzero, with modulus
$[S_L:S_L\cap L]^{-n/2}$, independent of $\Lambda$ and $N$.

Let $Q=\mathrm{LevQ}(D_w,l)^n$. Its order is $C$:
$\mathfrak b\bar{\mathfrak b}=(M)$ gives
$|\mathcal O_E/\mathfrak b|=M^{[E:\mathbb Q]/2}$,
and the original marked module has rank $r$.
The original finite Fourier pairing gives, for the prescribed
half-level roots $Y_q$ and allowed marked representative $c$,
\begin{equation}\label{raw-span-finite-fourier}
 \sum_{q\in Q}e^{-2\pi i\sum_b e(c_b,Y_{q,b})}
                   H(Y_q)p
       =C\,H(c)P_{\Lambda\otimes S_L}p,
 \qquad p=P_{\Lambda\otimes L}v.
\end{equation}
Here $Y_q$ represent
$(\Lambda\otimes S_L)/(\Lambda\otimes S_L\cap
\Lambda\otimes L)$. Their original odd lifts have
semicharacter one: multiplication by two is invertible on this
finite quotient. Averaging these lifts is the stated projection;
commuting $H(c)$ through the sum gives exactly the displayed
negative sign. The dual marking identifies the Fourier
coefficients $\widehat{1_{x_0}}(q)$ with these characters, up
to a single unit scalar fixed by the component marking. This
scalar is independent of $\Lambda,N$ because changing the
allowed representatives changes each alternating pairing by an
integer. It is retained below.

Combine \eqref{raw-span-projection} and
\eqref{raw-span-finite-fourier} with the literal raw reading
\eqref{eq:actual-raw-theta-classical}. Its two indices satisfy
$J/I=m_\Lambda^r$. Hence, on the $i$th component, the result is
\begin{equation}\label{raw-span-reading}
 \operatorname{rd}_U\mathcal T_{\Lambda,N,
          \widehat{1_{x_0}}}(P)(i,x)
   =A_i\,m_\Lambda^{r/2}\,
         \operatorname{thP}(\Phi_{L_i,\Lambda},P^\sigma)(g_i x),
                \qquad A_i\ne0.
\end{equation}
The coefficient $A_i$ retains the actual theta comparison,
the finite Fourier phase and the standard-vacuum overlap.
It depends on the component, but not on $\Lambda,N,P$.
In particular the factors $C$ cancel rather than being reset
to one. The original normalized theta dictionary makes the
comparison constant on each component. Equivalently, on unit
frames its modulus is fixed; its holomorphic ratio, where a
nonzero reading is available, has constant modulus and is
constant. Holomorphic continuation gives the same constant
for all readings. If all readings on a component vanish,
any nonzero constant supplies that vacuous identity.

\textbf{Spanning and descent of the polynomials.}
For a bounded form $F$ in $\operatorname{holSub}_0$, pull
$A_i^{-1}F_i$ to $L_i$ and extend it to its $\Delta$-orbit
using the original multiplier; put zero on the other orbits.
The stabilizer calculation makes this well-defined.
The modulus-one multiplier, the finite number of components,
and the original $\mathrm{ChiOne}$ condition give an element
of the exact bounded-family space used by the level spanning
conclusion in Section~\ref{actual-finite-theta-system}.
Apply that conclusion with $M_{\rm aux}=2bM$.
It gives a finite sum of the $\Phi_{L_i,\Lambda}$ with
$\Lambda\in\mathrm{Vset}(E,n,2bM)$ and row-highest harmonic
polynomials.

Each such $\Lambda$ is standard at $2bM$, so an odd $N$,
divisible by $M$, can be chosen with
$N\mathcal O_E^n\subset\Lambda\cap\mathcal O_E^n$.
This is the original $\mathrm{LevOK}$ condition. In each
fixed total degree, the harmonic and row-highest equations
are linear equations over $K$ on a finite-dimensional
polynomial space. Kernel formation commutes with extension
to $\mathbb C$. Clearing the finitely many coefficient
denominators and the powers of $N$ expresses every complex
solution as a finite complex combination of elements of
$\operatorname{harmL}_K(\Lambda,a)$; it does not change
the harmonic equations or $K$.
Apply \eqref{raw-span-reading} to those polynomials and
divide their coefficients by the positive
$m_\Lambda^{r/2}$. The actual raw sections from
Theorem~\ref{thm:actual-raw-theta-existence} then give the
required reading of $F$, simultaneously on every component.
Both readings are zero off the frame domain by their original
definitions. The empty-component case is immediate.
\end{proof}

This closes the actual raw spanning supplier. It does not bound
the chosen integral polynomial generators or the spanning
coefficients uniformly. The exact factors
$m_\Lambda^{r/2}$ and $A_i$ have been retained; no estimate
for the short integral families $h_0,h_{\rm sh}$ or for the
remaining selected-prime theta maps follows from qualitative
spanning alone.
