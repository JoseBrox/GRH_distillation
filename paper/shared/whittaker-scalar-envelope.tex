\paragraph{An explicit scalar bound for the full period sum.}
Keep the original unitary group, its common unipotent subgroup \(N\),
the stabilizers \(S_u=N\rtimes H_u\) for a fixed finite set of words, and
\(\Delta_u=S_u(F)\). Write
\(Y=G(F)\backslash G(\mathbb A_F)\), with its specified Haar measure.
Let \(K\subset G(\mathbb A_{F,f})\) be the original compact open
level, with unitary character \(\chi_K\), and let \(x_u\) be the
specified finite translate. Set
\[
 U_u=N_f\cap x_uKx_u^{-1},\qquad
 K_{H,u}=H_{u,f}\cap x_uKx_u^{-1},\qquad v_K=\operatorname{vol}(K).
\]
Assume the actual finite-level splitting
\(S_{u,f}\cap x_uKx_u^{-1}=U_u\rtimes K_{H,u}\), finitely many
representatives \(h_{u,\rho}\) for
\(H_u(F)\backslash H_{u,f}/K_{H,u}\), and trivial stabilizers
\(H_u(F)\cap h_{u,\rho}K_{H,u}h_{u,\rho}^{-1}=\{1\}\).
Retain the original character compatibility. For every column vector,
assume its period series converges absolutely almost everywhere and
its sum belongs to \(L^2(Y)\).

Let \(\pi_u\) be the specified irreducible unitary representation of
the compact group \(H_{u,\infty}\), of dimension \(d_u>0\), and let
\(W_u\) be its original Whittaker vector. Use compatible measures
\(dn_u,dh_u,d\nu_u\) on \(N_\infty,H_{u,\infty}\) and
\(S_{u,\infty}\backslash G_\infty\), so that
\[
 W_u(nhg)=\chi_u(n)\pi_u(h)W_u(g),\qquad
 \int\|W_u\|^2d\nu_u=1,\qquad
 a_u=\int\|W_u\|d\nu_u<\infty.
\]
The original column norm is
\(\|T\|^2=\sum_\rho\|\ell_{T,\rho}\|^2\), where
\(\ell_{T,\rho}\in V_u^*\). Its scalar seed satisfies
\[
 \Phi_u(T)(g,h_{u,\rho}x_uk)
   =\chi_K(k)\ell_{T,\rho}(W_u(g)),
\]
and is supported on \(G_\infty\times N_fH_{u,f}x_uK\).

\begin{lemma}[The exact seed normalization]
\label{shared-exact-seed-normalization}
Put
\[
 \Lambda_{u,h}=\{n\in N(F):n_f\in hU_uh^{-1}\},\qquad
 v_{N,u}=\operatorname{vol}(\Lambda_{u,1}\backslash N_\infty),
 \qquad v_{H,u}=\operatorname{vol}(H_{u,\infty}).
\]
Every \(\Lambda_{u,h}\) has the same positive finite covolume
\(v_{N,u}\). For \(c_u=v_Kv_{N,u}v_{H,u}/d_u\),
\[
 \int_{\Delta_u\backslash G(\mathbb A_F)}|\Phi_u(T)|^2
       =c_u\|T\|^2.
\]
Thus the normalized retained seed mass is its total column rank.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
On the Lie algebra of \(N\), conjugation by \(h\in H_u\) acts only
on the blocks \(B\in\operatorname{Hom}_E(W,X)\), by
\(B\mapsto Bh^{-1}\). The other blocks are fixed. Its determinant
over \(F\) is \(\operatorname{Norm}_{E/F}(\det_Eh)^{-r}=1\),
so it preserves unipotent Haar measure at every place.
Unipotent strong approximation gives \(N_f=N(F)hU_uh^{-1}\).
One can see this directly from polynomial logarithm and exponential
and approximation in the Lie algebra. A product fundamental domain gives
\[
 \operatorname{vol}(N(F)\backslash N(\mathbb A_F))
 =\operatorname{vol}(\Lambda_{u,h}\backslash N_\infty)
                  \operatorname{vol}(hU_uh^{-1}).
\]
The finite factor is independent of \(h\). Compactness of the
unipotent adelic quotient follows from its additive central filtration.

The finite-level splitting and trivial stabilizers decompose the seed
support into the cells
\((\Lambda_{u,h_{u,\rho}}\backslash G_\infty)
\times h_{u,\rho}x_uK\), with product quotient measure.
Indeed, reduce the finite Levi component to \(h_{u,\rho}\), remove
the finite unipotent component by strong approximation, and observe
that overlap forces the same \(\rho\) and then a trivial rational
Levi component. The remaining ambiguity is exactly \(\Lambda_{u,h_{u,\rho}}\).
Disintegration of each cell and compact Schur orthogonality give
\[
 \int_H|\ell(\pi_u(h)w)|^2dh_u
       =\frac{v_{H,u}}{d_u}\|\ell\|^2\|w\|^2.
\]
For this last identity, average the rank-one operator of \(w\) under
the irreducible representation and take its trace. The remaining
factors are \(v_K,v_{N,u}\) and \(\int\|W_u\|^2=1\).
\end{proof}

Let \(P_u\) be the full period map,
\(P_uT(y)=\sum_{\Delta_u\backslash G(F)}\Phi_u(T)(\delta y)\).
Define the nonnegative arithmetic sum
\[
 F(y)=\sum_u a_u\sum_{\delta\in\Delta_u\backslash G(F)}
 \mathbf1_{N_fH_{u,f}x_uK}((\delta y)_f)
                  \|W_u((\delta y)_\infty)\|.
\]
All rational cosets and the original finite supports occur here.
Tonelli's theorem and the same cell decomposition give
\[
 \int_YF=\sum_u c_u\dim(C_u)a_u^2<\infty.
\]

\begin{theorem}[A scalar sufficient bound]
\label{shared-whittaker-scalar-envelope}
Let \(J_u:C_u^0\hookrightarrow C_u\) and \(\Pi\) be the actual
isometric source restrictions and orthogonal target projection. Write
\(H_0=\operatorname{im}\Pi\), \(n=\dim H_0>0\), and
\[
 Q_0=\sum_u c_u^{-1}\Pi P_uJ_uJ_u^*P_u^*\Pi,
 \qquad B_0(y)=\sum_{j=1}^n|e_j(y)|^2,
\]
where \((e_j)\) is an orthonormal basis of \(H_0\). For every
\(\alpha>0\) with finite right side,
\begin{equation}\label{shared-whittaker-excess}
 \operatorname{tr}(Q_0-\alpha I)_+
       \le\int_Y(F-\alpha)_+B_0.
\end{equation}
The kernel \(B_0\) is basis-independent and has integral \(n\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
For one word, put
\(p_u(g)=\mathbf1_{N_fH_{u,f}x_uK}(g_f)\|W_u(g_\infty)\|\).
The same Schur and cell calculation gives the exact weighted identity
\[
 \int_{\Delta_u\backslash G(\mathbb A_F)}
             \frac{|\Phi_u(T)|^2}{p_u}=c_ua_u\|T\|^2,
\]
with zero integrand where the seed vanishes. If \(\int p_u|h|^2\)
is finite, weighted Cauchy--Schwarz makes the pairing absolutely
integrable, so Tonelli's theorem justifies one unfolding. It then gives
\[
 c_u^{-1}\|P_u^*h\|^2
 \le a_u\int_{\Delta_u\backslash G(\mathbb A_F)}
     \mathbf1_{N_fH_{u,f}x_uK}(g_f)\|W_u(g_\infty)\|\,|h(g)|^2dg.
\]
Indeed, apply the weighted inequality to \(\langle P_uT,h\rangle\)
and take the supremum over unit \(T\). The bound is automatic when
the right side is infinite. No right-level condition on \(h\), global
\(L^2\) bound for the absolute fold, or orthogonality of words is used.
Restrictions are contractions. Summing and unfolding the positive
terms therefore gives \(\langle Q_0h,h\rangle\le\int F|h|^2\)
for \(h\in H_0\). Apply this inequality on the positive spectral
subspace of \(Q_0-\alpha I\). Its diagonal kernel lies between zero
and \(B_0\), which proves the result. Finiteness of the stated right
side implies \(\int FB_0<\infty\), so all compressed quadratic
forms are defined; no boundedness of \(F\) is required.
\end{proof}

At a prescribed \(c>0\), take \(\alpha=c/n\) and divide
\eqref{shared-whittaker-excess} by \(c\) to bound the target defect
in Theorem~\ref{shared-prescribed-trace}. The scalar integral still
requires an arithmetic estimate. The seed identity does not control
the source deficit after periodization: other rational orbits remain.
No change has been made to the integral maps, their heights, lattices
or restrictions, and no global norm estimate or nonvanishing is inferred
from the seed normalization alone.
